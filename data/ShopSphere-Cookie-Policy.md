# ShopSphere Cookie Policy

**Effective Date: July 23, 2026**
**Last Updated: July 23, 2026**

## 1. Introduction

1.1 This Cookie Policy explains how ShopSphere Commerce Pvt. Ltd. and its affiliates (collectively, "ShopSphere," "we," "us," or "our") use Cookies and similar tracking technologies on www.shopsphere.com, our mobile applications, and related services (collectively, the "Platform").

1.2 This Cookie Policy forms part of, and should be read together with, our Privacy Policy and Terms & Conditions, both of which are incorporated by reference and available on the Platform.

1.3 By using the Platform and interacting with our cookie consent banner, you can manage your preferences regarding non-essential Cookies as described in Section 7 below.

## 2. Definitions

2.1 "Cookies" means small text files placed on your device by a website or application when you visit it, which are widely used to make websites function, improve efficiency, and provide information to website owners.

2.2 "Similar Technologies" means technologies that store or access information on a User's device or otherwise collect or process information through means similar to Cookies, including web beacons, pixel tags, tracking scripts, software development kits (SDKs), and local storage objects.

2.3 "First-Party Cookies" means Cookies set directly by ShopSphere.

2.4 "Third-Party Cookies" means Cookies set by parties other than ShopSphere, such as advertising networks, analytics providers, and social media platforms integrated with the Platform.

2.5 "Session Cookies" means Cookies that are temporary and expire once you close your browser or app.

2.6 "Persistent Cookies" means Cookies that remain on your device for a set period, or until you delete them, and are activated each time you visit the Platform during that period.

2.7 Capitalized terms not otherwise defined in this Policy have the meanings given to them in our Privacy Policy and Terms & Conditions.

## 3. What Are Cookies and Similar Technologies

3.1 Cookies allow a website to recognize a User's device and store certain information about the User's preferences or past actions. Similar Technologies serve comparable functions and are used alongside Cookies to enhance functionality, security, and personalization.

3.2 On mobile applications, we and our partners may use SDKs and mobile advertising identifiers (such as Google's Advertising ID or Apple's IDFA) that function similarly to Cookies for tracking and analytics purposes.

3.3 We use both Session Cookies and Persistent Cookies, as well as both First-Party and Third-Party Cookies, on the Platform.

## 4. Why We Use Cookies

4.1 We use Cookies and Similar Technologies for the following purposes:

  4.1.1 To enable core Platform functionality, such as maintaining your Account session, remembering items in your shopping cart, and enabling secure checkout;

  4.1.2 To remember your preferences, such as language, currency, and display settings;

  4.1.3 To analyze how Users interact with the Platform, in order to improve performance, usability, and Content;

  4.1.4 To personalize your experience, including Product recommendations and search results;

  4.1.5 To measure the effectiveness of our marketing campaigns and advertisements, both on and off the Platform;

  4.1.6 To detect, prevent, and investigate fraud, security incidents, and violations of our Terms & Conditions;

  4.1.7 To enable social media features, such as sharing Products on social platforms.

## 5. Categories of Cookies We Use

5.1 **Strictly Necessary Cookies.** These Cookies are essential for the Platform to function and cannot be switched off in our systems. They are usually only set in response to actions you take, such as logging in, adding items to your cart, filling in forms, or setting privacy preferences. Examples include Cookies that maintain your session, enable secure checkout, and support load balancing. These Cookies do not require your consent, as they are necessary for delivering the Services you request under our Terms & Conditions.

5.2 **Performance and Analytics Cookies.** These Cookies collect information about how Users use the Platform, such as which pages are visited most often, how Users navigate between pages, and whether Users encounter error messages. This information helps us improve how the Platform works. Data collected by these Cookies is generally aggregated and used on a statistical basis. Examples include cookies set by analytics providers to measure page views, session duration, and bounce rates.

5.3 **Functionality Cookies.** These Cookies allow the Platform to remember choices you make, such as your username, language, region, or display preferences, to provide enhanced and more personalized features. They may also be used to remember changes you have made to text size, fonts, and other customizable parts of the Platform.

5.4 **Targeting and Advertising Cookies.** These Cookies are used to deliver advertisements more relevant to you and your interests, both on the Platform and on third-party websites and applications. They are also used to limit the number of times you see an advertisement and to help measure the effectiveness of advertising campaigns. These Cookies are usually placed by advertising networks with our permission and may track your browsing activity across other websites.

5.5 **Social Media Cookies.** These Cookies are set by social media services that we have added to the Platform to enable you to share Content with your networks. These Cookies may track your browser across other websites and build a profile of your interests, which may affect the content and messages you see on other websites you visit.

## 7. Managing Your Cookie Preferences

7.1 **Cookie Consent Banner.** When you first visit the Platform, you will be presented with a cookie consent banner allowing you to accept all Cookies, reject non-essential Cookies, or customize your preferences by category as described in Section 5.

7.2 **Cookie Preference Center.** You may update your Cookie preferences at any time by accessing the "Cookie Settings" link located in the footer of the Platform. Changes to your preferences will apply to future Cookie placement and, where technically feasible, will result in removal of previously set non-essential Cookies.

7.3 **Browser Controls.** Most web browsers allow you to control Cookies through browser settings, including blocking or deleting Cookies. Please note that if you disable Cookies, certain features of the Platform, including maintaining items in your shopping cart, remaining logged in, and personalized recommendations, may not function properly. Instructions for managing Cookies in common browsers can typically be found in each browser's help documentation.

7.4 **Mobile Device Controls.** On mobile devices, you can manage advertising identifiers and tracking permissions through your device settings, such as "Limit Ad Tracking" (iOS) or "Opt out of Ads Personalization" (Android).

7.5 **Third-Party Opt-Outs.** For Third-Party Cookies used for advertising purposes, you may also opt out directly through industry tools such as the Digital Advertising Alliance's opt-out page, the Network Advertising Initiative's opt-out page, or the European Interactive Digital Advertising Alliance's opt-out platform, where applicable to your region.

7.6 **Do Not Track Signals.** Some browsers offer a "Do Not Track" (DNT) feature. As there is currently no industry-wide standard for interpreting DNT signals, the Platform does not currently respond to DNT signals, though you can still manage your Cookie preferences as described in this Section 7.
