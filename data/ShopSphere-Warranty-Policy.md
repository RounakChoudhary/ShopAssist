# ShopSphere Warranty Policy

**Effective Date: July 23, 2026**
**Last Updated: July 23, 2026**

## 1. Introduction

1.1 This Warranty Policy describes the warranty coverage applicable to Products purchased through www.shopsphere.com and our mobile applications (collectively, the "Platform"), including manufacturer warranties, ShopSphere-provided warranties where applicable, and optional extended warranty programs.

1.2 This Policy should be read together with our Terms & Conditions, Return Policy, Refund Policy, and Shipping Policy, all of which are available on the Platform and incorporated by reference.

1.3 Warranty coverage is separate and distinct from the Return Window described in our Return Policy. The Return Window addresses your right to return a Product shortly after delivery for a variety of reasons, whereas warranty coverage addresses remedies for defects that manifest during ownership, typically after the Return Window has closed.

## 2. Definitions

2.1 "Warranty" means a manufacturer's or ShopSphere's assurance that a Product will be free from defects in materials and workmanship for a specified period, and that such defects will be remedied by repair, Replacement, or refund, subject to the exclusions in this Policy.

2.2 "Manufacturer Warranty" means a Warranty provided directly by the Product's manufacturer or brand owner, the terms of which are typically included with the Product at the time of delivery or made available on the manufacturer's website.

2.3 "ShopSphere Warranty" means a Warranty provided directly by ShopSphere for select Products, typically private-label or ShopSphere-branded Products, as disclosed on the applicable Product listing page.

2.4 "Extended Warranty" means an optional, separately purchased Warranty program that extends coverage beyond the standard Manufacturer Warranty or ShopSphere Warranty period, or that broadens the scope of coverage.

2.5 "Warranty Period" means the duration during which Warranty coverage is active for a given Product, commencing from the date of delivery unless otherwise specified by the manufacturer or ShopSphere.

2.6 "Authorized Service Center" means a repair facility designated by the manufacturer, ShopSphere, or an Extended Warranty provider to perform Warranty repairs.

2.7 Capitalized terms not otherwise defined in this Policy have the meanings given to them in our Terms & Conditions and Return Policy.

## 3. Types of Warranty Coverage

3.1 **Manufacturer Warranty.** Most Products sold on the Platform, particularly electronics, appliances, and branded goods, carry a Manufacturer Warranty. The duration and terms of the Manufacturer Warranty are set by the manufacturer and are disclosed on the Product listing page or included in the Product packaging. ShopSphere does not modify or extend Manufacturer Warranty terms unless explicitly stated.

3.2 **ShopSphere Warranty.** For Products sold under ShopSphere's own private-label brands, ShopSphere provides a direct Warranty, the terms and duration of which are disclosed on the applicable Product listing page. Unless otherwise specified, ShopSphere Warranty coverage is 12 months from the date of delivery.

3.3 **No Warranty Products.** Certain Product categories, including but not limited to consumables, perishable goods, and Products explicitly marked "Sold As-Is" or "No Warranty" on the Product listing page, do not carry a Warranty, though such Products remain subject to the Return Policy and applicable consumer protection law regarding fitness for purpose at the time of sale.

3.4 **Marketplace Seller Products.** For Products sold by Marketplace Sellers, Warranty coverage is typically provided by the Product manufacturer rather than the Marketplace Seller or ShopSphere directly, except where a Marketplace Seller explicitly offers its own seller warranty, which will be disclosed on the Product listing page.

## 4. Warranty Registration

4.1 For certain Products, Warranty coverage may require registration with the manufacturer within a specified period after delivery, typically 30 days, via the manufacturer's website or included registration card. Failure to register within the required period may affect your ability to claim Warranty service directly with the manufacturer, though your original ShopSphere Order confirmation and invoice generally serve as adequate proof of purchase for Warranty purposes in most jurisdictions.

4.2 ShopSphere recommends retaining your Order confirmation, tax invoice, and original packaging for the duration of the Warranty Period, as these documents are typically required to process a Warranty claim.

## 5. Extended Warranty Programs

5.1 For select Product categories, primarily electronics, appliances, and mobile devices, ShopSphere offers optional Extended Warranty programs at the time of purchase, provided by ShopSphere or a third-party Extended Warranty provider, as disclosed at checkout.

5.2 Extended Warranty programs may provide coverage beyond the standard Manufacturer Warranty period, additional coverage for accidental damage (where explicitly included), or expedited service benefits such as doorstep repair or advance Replacement.

5.3 Extended Warranty plans must generally be purchased at the same time as the underlying Product, or within a limited window thereafter (typically 15 days), as specified in the Extended Warranty terms presented at the time of offer.

5.4 Extended Warranty coverage is governed by the specific terms and conditions of the applicable Extended Warranty provider, which will be provided to you at the time of purchase and are supplementary to, and do not replace, this Policy or the standard Manufacturer Warranty.

5.5 Extended Warranty plans are generally non-transferable and are cancellable within 30 days of purchase for a prorated refund, less the value of any service already availed, unless otherwise specified in the Extended Warranty terms.

## 6. Filing a Warranty Claim

6.1 To initiate a Warranty claim, log in to your Account, navigate to "My Orders," select the relevant Product, and choose the "Warranty Claim" option, or contact customer support at support@shopsphere.com with your Order number and a description of the defect.

6.2 Depending on the Product and the nature of the defect, you may be directed to: (a) an Authorized Service Center for direct manufacturer Warranty service; (b) ShopSphere's own Warranty claim process for ShopSphere Warranty-covered Products; or (c) the applicable Extended Warranty provider's claim process.

6.3 You may be required to provide photographic or video evidence of the defect, proof of purchase, and, in some cases, the Product's serial number or IMEI, to process the Warranty claim.

6.4 For Products still within the Return Window described in our Return Policy, a defect discovered shortly after delivery may be addressed either as a Return under Section 4 of that Policy or as a Warranty claim under this Policy, at ShopSphere's reasonable discretion, generally whichever provides the faster resolution for you.

## 7. Warranty Remedies

7.1 Depending on the nature of the defect, the applicable Warranty provider (manufacturer, ShopSphere, or Extended Warranty provider) will offer one of the following remedies, at its discretion, unless the specific Warranty terms specify otherwise:

  7.1.1 **Repair:** The Product will be repaired, either at an Authorized Service Center or via doorstep service where available, and returned to you;

  7.1.2 **Replacement:** The Product will be replaced with an identical new or refurbished unit of equivalent specification, where repair is not feasible or economical;

  7.1.3 **Refund:** Where neither repair nor Replacement is feasible (such as due to Product discontinuation, as described in Section 7 of our Product Availability Policy), a refund may be issued, calculated based on the Product's original purchase price, potentially adjusted for depreciation based on the age of the Product and duration of use, in accordance with applicable law and the specific Warranty terms.

7.2 Warranty repair timelines vary by Product category and Authorized Service Center capacity, but ShopSphere and its Warranty partners aim to complete standard repairs within 7–15 business days of Product receipt at the Authorized Service Center, excluding time required for parts procurement in the case of less common components.

7.3 If a Warranty repair cannot be completed within the standard timeline due to parts unavailability, you will be notified of the delay and, where the delay is likely to exceed 30 days, may be offered a Replacement or refund as an alternative remedy, subject to the applicable Warranty provider's policies.

## 8. Warranty Exclusions

8.1 Warranty coverage, whether Manufacturer Warranty, ShopSphere Warranty, or Extended Warranty, generally does not cover:

  8.1.1 Damage caused by misuse, negligence, accident, or improper installation, except where Extended Warranty coverage explicitly includes accidental damage protection;

  8.1.2 Normal wear and tear, including cosmetic damage such as scratches, dents, or discoloration that does not affect functionality;

  8.1.3 Damage caused by unauthorized repair, modification, or use of non-genuine parts or accessories;

  8.1.4 Damage resulting from exposure to liquids, moisture, or extreme environmental conditions, unless the Product is specifically rated for such exposure and the damage occurred within rated tolerances;

  8.1.5 Damage caused by power surges, incorrect voltage, or improper electrical connections;

  8.1.6 Consumable components with a limited expected lifespan, such as batteries, filters, and bulbs, beyond their expected service life, unless defective upon delivery;

  8.1.7 Software issues, data loss, or damage caused by malware, unless directly caused by a manufacturing defect in the Product's original firmware or embedded software;

  8.1.8 Products with tampered, removed, or illegible serial numbers or warranty seals;

  8.1.9 Products used for commercial or industrial purposes where the Product is designated by the manufacturer for personal or household use only;

  8.1.10 Defects arising from failure to follow the manufacturer's care and usage instructions provided with the Product;

  8.1.11 Products purchased from unauthorized or grey-market channels, which are not applicable to Products purchased directly through the Platform in the ordinary course.

8.2 The specific exclusions applicable to a given Product are governed by the terms of its Manufacturer Warranty, ShopSphere Warranty, or Extended Warranty, as applicable, which may include additional or different exclusions disclosed at the time of purchase.

## 9. Warranty Claims Involving Marketplace Sellers

9.1 Where a Product is sold by a Marketplace Seller and carries a Manufacturer Warranty, ShopSphere will assist in facilitating your Warranty claim with the manufacturer or its Authorized Service Center, but the Marketplace Seller is not typically responsible for honoring the Manufacturer Warranty directly, unless the Marketplace Seller has explicitly offered its own seller warranty.

9.2 If a Marketplace Seller has misrepresented Warranty coverage for a Product (for example, falsely claiming a Manufacturer Warranty that does not exist), please report this to marketplace-integrity@shopsphere.com; ShopSphere will investigate and, where confirmed, facilitate an appropriate remedy for you and take corrective action against the Marketplace Seller in accordance with our seller policies.

## 10. International Warranty Considerations

10.1 For Products purchased for international delivery, as described in Section 8 of our Shipping Policy, Manufacturer Warranty coverage may be limited to the country of original sale or may require you to arrange service through an Authorized Service Center in your country of residence, depending on the manufacturer's international warranty policy. This information, where available, will be disclosed on the Product listing page.

10.2 ShopSphere does not guarantee that Manufacturer Warranty service will be available in all countries to which we ship, and you are encouraged to review the manufacturer's international warranty terms before purchasing a Product for international delivery.

## 11. Relationship to Return and Refund Policies

11.1 If a Product defect is discovered within the Return Window described in our Return Policy, you may generally choose between initiating a Return under Section 4 of that Policy (for a refund, Replacement, or Exchange) or filing a Warranty claim under this Policy, as described in Section 6.4 above.

11.2 If a Product defect is discovered after the Return Window has expired, but within the applicable Warranty Period, you must proceed via a Warranty claim under this Policy rather than the Return Policy. Refunds issued as a Warranty remedy under Section 7.1.3 are processed in accordance with the refund modes and timelines described in our Refund Policy, adjusted for any applicable depreciation as described in Section 7.1.3.

## 12. Frequently Encountered Edge Cases

12.1 **Defect Discovered Just After Return Window Closes:** You should file a Warranty claim under this Policy rather than a Return; provided the defect is a genuine manufacturing defect and not wear and tear or misuse, standard Warranty remedies under Section 7 apply.

12.2 **Product Fails During Repair Under Warranty:** If a Product is damaged further due to mishandling by an Authorized Service Center during a Warranty repair, this will be treated as a service center error, and a full Replacement or refund will be offered at no cost to you, separate from standard Warranty remedy limitations.

12.3 **Multiple Warranty Claims for the Same Defect:** If a Product exhibits the same defect after a Warranty repair, you may request a Replacement rather than a further repair, particularly where the Product has already undergone 2 or more repair attempts for the same underlying issue.

12.4 **Warranty Claim for a Gifted Product:** The recipient of a gifted Product may file a Warranty claim using the original Order confirmation and invoice provided by the purchasing User, or by providing the Product's serial number and proof of the gift, where the original Order details are not available to the recipient.

12.5 **Extended Warranty Purchased but Product Returned Under Return Policy:** If the underlying Product is returned within the Return Window, any associated Extended Warranty purchased alongside it will be automatically cancelled and refunded in full, as the Extended Warranty cannot exist independently of the underlying Product.

12.6 **Manufacturer Ceases Operations During Warranty Period:** Where a Product's manufacturer ceases operations or discontinues Authorized Service Center support during the Warranty Period, and the Product carries a ShopSphere Warranty or was covered by an Extended Warranty, ShopSphere or the Extended Warranty provider will make reasonable efforts to arrange alternative service or offer a refund remedy under Section 7.1.3, as applicable; for Products covered solely by a Manufacturer Warranty with no ShopSphere or Extended Warranty component, ShopSphere's ability to assist is limited, as such Warranty is a direct arrangement between you and the manufacturer.

13.2 For ShopSphere Warranty and Extended Warranty claims, ShopSphere's or the Extended Warranty provider's liability is limited to the remedies described in Section 7 (repair, Replacement, or refund), and neither ShopSphere nor any Extended Warranty provider shall be liable for indirect or consequential damages arising from a Product defect, except where such exclusion is not permitted under applicable law.

13.3 Nothing in this Policy affects any non-excludable statutory rights available to you under the Consumer Protection Act, 2019, or equivalent legislation in your jurisdiction, including rights relating to defective goods that may exist independently of any Warranty.
