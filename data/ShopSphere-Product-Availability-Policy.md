**Effective Date: July 23, 2026**
**Last Updated: July 23, 2026**

## 1. Introduction

1.1 This Product Availability Policy explains how ShopSphere Commerce Pvt. Ltd. and its affiliates (collectively, "ShopSphere," "we," "us," or "our") represent, manage, and communicate the availability of Products listed on www.shopsphere.com and our mobile applications (collectively, the "Platform").

1.2 This Policy forms part of, and should be read together with, our Terms & Conditions, particularly Section 6 (Orders) and Section 7 (Pricing and Payment), as well as our Privacy Policy and Cookie Policy, all of which are incorporated by reference and available on the Platform.

1.3 By placing an Order through the Platform, you acknowledge and agree to the terms of this Product Availability Policy.

## 2. Definitions

2.1 "In Stock" means a Product is available for immediate fulfillment and dispatch within the timeframe indicated on the Product listing page.

2.2 "Out of Stock" means a Product is temporarily unavailable for purchase and cannot presently be ordered.

2.3 "Backorder" means a Product that is temporarily unavailable but which Users may still order, with fulfillment to occur once inventory is replenished, subject to Section 5.

2.4 "Pre-Order" means a Product listed for purchase prior to its official release or restock date, with fulfillment occurring on or after the announced availability date.

2.5 "Discontinued" means a Product that ShopSphere or the relevant Marketplace Seller has permanently ceased offering for sale.

2.6 "Marketplace Seller" has the meaning given in our Terms & Conditions, referring to third-party sellers who list and sell Products through the Platform.

2.7 "Inventory Management System" means the technology systems used by ShopSphere and Marketplace Sellers to track, update, and synchronize Product stock levels across the Platform.

2.8 Capitalized terms not otherwise defined in this Policy have the meanings given to them in our Terms & Conditions.

## 3. Representation of Product Availability

3.1 ShopSphere strives to display accurate, real-time Product availability information on the Platform based on data provided by our own warehouses and by Marketplace Sellers through our Inventory Management System.

3.2 Availability statuses displayed on the Platform, including "In Stock," "Out of Stock," "Limited Stock," "Backorder," and "Pre-Order," are indicative and updated on a periodic basis. Due to the dynamic nature of e-commerce, factors such as concurrent Orders, processing delays, and synchronization intervals between Marketplace Seller inventory systems and the Platform may result in temporary discrepancies between displayed availability and actual availability.

3.3 ShopSphere does not guarantee that any Product displayed as "In Stock" will remain available at the time an Order is placed or confirmed. Availability is confirmed only upon successful Order placement and receipt of Order confirmation, as described in Section 6.1 of our Terms & Conditions.

3.4 Product listings may display estimated delivery windows based on current stock levels, shipping origin, and destination. These estimates are approximate and subject to the limitations described in Section 6.5 of our Terms & Conditions.

## 4. Out-of-Stock Products

4.1 If a Product becomes unavailable after you have added it to your cart but before you complete checkout, the Product will be automatically removed from your cart or flagged as unavailable, and you will be notified accordingly at checkout.

4.2 If a Product becomes unavailable after your Order has been placed but before it has been dispatched, ShopSphere or the relevant Marketplace Seller will notify you as soon as reasonably practicable via email or Platform notification, and will:

  4.2.1 Cancel the affected line item and issue a full refund for that item in accordance with Section 7.6 of our Terms & Conditions; or

  4.2.2 Where reasonably comparable, offer a substitute Product of equal or greater value at no additional cost, subject to your acceptance; or

  4.2.3 Offer the option to wait for the Product to be restocked, where a reasonably certain restock date is available, subject to Section 5 (Backorders).

4.3 You will not be charged for any Product that is ultimately not fulfilled due to unavailability. Where payment has already been captured, ShopSphere will process a refund in accordance with Section 7.6 of our Terms & Conditions.

4.4 Users may opt to receive "Back in Stock" notifications for Out of Stock Products via email or push notification by selecting the relevant option on the Product listing page. Provision of such notifications does not guarantee availability at the time of notification, as stock may be claimed by other Users before you complete your purchase.

## 5. Backorders

5.1 Certain Products may be made available for Backorder, allowing you to place an Order despite temporary unavailability, with fulfillment upon restock. Where a Product is offered on Backorder, the Product listing page will clearly indicate this status along with an estimated restock and dispatch date, where available.

5.2 Estimated restock dates for Backordered Products are provided by ShopSphere or the relevant Marketplace Seller based on available supply chain information and are subject to change without notice due to factors including manufacturing delays, supplier constraints, and logistics disruptions.

5.3 You may cancel a Backordered Product at any time prior to dispatch by accessing "My Orders" in your Account, in accordance with Section 6.3 of our Terms & Conditions, and will receive a full refund of any amount paid for that item.

5.4 If a Backordered Product's estimated restock date is delayed by more than thirty (30) days from the original estimate, ShopSphere will notify affected Users and provide the option to cancel the Order for a full refund or continue waiting for fulfillment.

## 6. Pre-Orders

6.1 Products available for Pre-Order will be clearly labeled as such, along with the anticipated release or availability date, which is provided on a best-efforts basis and subject to change.

6.2 Payment for Pre-Order Products may be collected at the time of Order placement or upon dispatch, as specified on the Product listing page. Where payment is collected at the time of Order placement, ShopSphere will hold such funds in accordance with applicable law until the Product is dispatched.

6.3 If a Pre-Order Product's release date is delayed, ShopSphere will make reasonable efforts to notify affected Users of the updated timeline. If the delay exceeds sixty (60) days from the originally announced release date, Users will be given the option to cancel their Pre-Order for a full refund.

6.4 If a Pre-Order Product is cancelled by ShopSphere or the relevant Marketplace Seller prior to release, for any reason, including but not limited to manufacturer cancellation or discontinuation, affected Users will be notified and issued a full refund.

## 7. Discontinued Products

7.1 ShopSphere and Marketplace Sellers may discontinue Products at their discretion due to factors including manufacturer discontinuation, regulatory changes, seasonal rotation, or business decisions.

7.2 Discontinued Products will be removed from active listings on the Platform or clearly marked as "Discontinued" and unavailable for further ordering.

7.3 Discontinuation of a Product does not affect the validity of any warranty, return, or refund rights applicable to units of that Product already purchased, which remain governed by the applicable Returns and Refunds Policy and any manufacturer warranty terms in effect at the time of purchase.

7.4 ShopSphere does not guarantee the continued availability of replacement parts, accessories, or consumables for Discontinued Products beyond what is separately committed to by the relevant manufacturer or Marketplace Seller.

## 8. Limited Stock and High-Demand Products

8.1 For Products with limited inventory relative to anticipated demand, the Platform may display a "Limited Stock" or similar indicator, along with, where available, an approximate remaining quantity. Such indicators are provided to assist Users in making informed purchasing decisions and are updated periodically but may not reflect real-time inventory with complete precision.

8.2 ShopSphere reserves the right to impose reasonable purchase quantity limits per Account or household for high-demand Products, promotional items, or Products subject to supply constraints, in order to ensure fair access for all Users. Any applicable limits will be disclosed on the Product listing page or at checkout.

8.3 ShopSphere reserves the right to cancel Orders that it reasonably believes were placed in violation of applicable quantity limits, including Orders placed using multiple Accounts by the same individual or household, in accordance with Section 6.2 of our Terms & Conditions.

## 9. Marketplace Seller Inventory

9.1 Where Products are listed and fulfilled by Marketplace Sellers rather than ShopSphere directly, availability information is provided by the Marketplace Seller through the Inventory Management System. While ShopSphere requires Marketplace Sellers to maintain accurate and up-to-date inventory information as a condition of their seller agreement, ShopSphere does not independently verify real-time stock levels held by Marketplace Sellers and, consistent with Section 10.3 of our Terms & Conditions, does not guarantee the accuracy of Marketplace Seller-provided availability data.

9.2 If a Marketplace Seller fails to fulfill an Order due to inaccurate inventory representation, ShopSphere will facilitate cancellation of the affected Order and full refund to the User, and may take corrective action against the Marketplace Seller in accordance with our seller policies, up to and including suspension or termination of the seller's access to the Platform.
