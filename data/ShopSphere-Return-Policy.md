# ShopSphere Return Policy

**Effective Date: July 23, 2026**
**Last Updated: July 23, 2026**

## 1. Introduction

1.1 This Return Policy describes the circumstances under which Users may return Products purchased through www.shopsphere.com and our mobile applications (collectively, the "Platform"), and the procedures for initiating and completing such returns.

1.2 This Policy should be read together with our Terms & Conditions, Shipping Policy, Refund Policy, Cancellation Policy, and Warranty Policy, all of which are available on the Platform and incorporated by reference.

1.3 This Policy applies to Products sold both directly by ShopSphere and by Marketplace Sellers through the Platform, subject to any Product-specific return terms disclosed on the relevant Product listing page, which take precedence over general provisions of this Policy where more specific.

## 2. Definitions

2.1 "Return" means the process by which a User sends back a purchased Product to ShopSphere or a Marketplace Seller in exchange for a refund or Exchange.

2.2 "Return Window" means the period, beginning on the date of delivery, during which a User may initiate a Return for an eligible Product.

2.3 "Exchange" means the process by which a returned Product is replaced with a different size, color, variant, or an entirely different Product, subject to price adjustment where applicable.

2.4 "Replacement" means the process by which a defective or damaged Product is replaced with an identical new unit of the same Product.

2.5 "Non-Returnable Product" means a Product category or specific Product that is excluded from the Return Window due to its nature, as described in Section 5.

2.6 "Reverse Pickup" means the collection of a returned Product from the User's address by a Courier Partner, as arranged through the Platform.

2.7 Capitalized terms not otherwise defined in this Policy have the meanings given to them in our Terms & Conditions, Shipping Policy, and Product Availability Policy.

## 3. Return Eligibility

3.1 Subject to the exclusions in Section 5, most Products purchased through the Platform are eligible for Return within a Return Window specified on the Product listing page, typically between 7 and 15 days from the date of delivery, unless a longer or shorter period is indicated for a specific Product or category.

3.2 To be eligible for Return, a Product must generally meet the following conditions, except where the Return is due to a defect, damage, or seller error as described in Section 4:

  3.2.1 The Product must be unused, unworn, and in its original condition;

  3.2.2 The Product must include all original tags, labels, accessories, manuals, and packaging;

  3.2.3 The Product must not show signs of installation, alteration, or use beyond reasonable inspection consistent with Section 15 of our Shipping Policy (Open-Box Delivery);

  3.2.4 For electronics and appliances, original seals, warranty cards, and serial number stickers must be intact.

3.3 ShopSphere and Marketplace Sellers reserve the right to inspect returned Products upon receipt and to decline a Return, Exchange, or associated refund if the Product does not meet the eligibility conditions in Section 3.2, in which case the Product will be returned to the User at ShopSphere's discretion, and you will be notified of the reason for rejection.

3.4 Return eligibility, including the applicable Return Window and any category-specific conditions, is displayed on each Product listing page at the time of purchase and forms part of the terms applicable to that specific Order.

## 4. Returns Due to Defects, Damage, or Seller Error

4.1 If a Product is received damaged in transit, as described in Section 13 of our Shipping Policy, defective on arrival, materially different from its description on the Platform, or if you receive an incorrect Product due to a fulfillment error, you may initiate a Return within the applicable Return Window, or within 48 hours of delivery for transit damage claims, whichever provides the longer period, regardless of whether the Product would otherwise be classified as a Non-Returnable Product under Section 5.

4.2 For Returns under this Section 4, ShopSphere or the relevant Marketplace Seller will arrange free Reverse Pickup, and you will not be charged any restocking or return shipping fee. You will be offered the choice of a Replacement, Exchange, or full refund in accordance with our Refund Policy.

4.3 You may be asked to provide photographic or video evidence of the defect, damage, or discrepancy to expedite processing of your Return request.

## 5. Non-Returnable Products

5.1 Certain categories of Products are not eligible for Return due to hygiene, safety, customization, or regulatory considerations, except where the Return arises from a defect, damage, or seller error as described in Section 4. Non-Returnable Products generally include:

  5.1.1 Innerwear, lingerie, and swimwear, for hygiene reasons;

  5.1.2 Cosmetics, personal care products, and perishable goods, once opened or with broken seals;

  5.1.3 Customized, personalized, or made-to-order Products, including engraved items or made-to-measure apparel;

  5.1.4 Digital goods, gift cards, and downloadable software, once activated or redeemed;

  5.1.5 Perishable food items and items with expiration dates that have been delivered in acceptable condition;

  5.1.6 Products explicitly marked as "Non-Returnable" on the Product listing page at the time of purchase;

  5.1.7 Products purchased during certain clearance or final-sale promotional events, where clearly disclosed as final sale at the time of purchase;

  5.1.8 Installed or activated software licenses and subscriptions.

5.2 The applicability of Non-Returnable status to a specific Product will be clearly disclosed on the Product listing page prior to purchase, consistent with Section 3.4.

## 6. Initiating a Return

6.1 To initiate a Return, log in to your Account, navigate to "My Orders," select the relevant Order and Product, and choose the "Return" option, specifying the reason for the Return from the available categories.

6.2 Upon submission, you will receive confirmation of your Return request, along with instructions for Reverse Pickup scheduling, as described in Section 8, or, where Reverse Pickup is not available in your area, instructions for self-shipping the Product back to the designated return address.

6.3 Where self-shipping is required, ShopSphere or the relevant Marketplace Seller will reimburse reasonable return shipping costs for Returns under Section 4 (Defects, Damage, or Seller Error), but not for Returns initiated for reasons of preference under Section 3, unless otherwise stated on the Product listing page.

## 7. Exchange Process

7.1 Where available for a given Product, you may request an Exchange in lieu of a refund, for a different size, color, or variant of the same Product, subject to availability under our Product Availability Policy.

7.2 If the Exchange item has a different price than the original Product, you will be required to pay the price difference, or will receive a refund of the difference, as applicable, processed in accordance with our Refund Policy.

7.3 Exchange requests are subject to the same eligibility conditions described in Section 3.2, unless the Exchange is requested under Section 4 (Defects, Damage, or Seller Error), in which case no additional conditions apply beyond confirming the defect or discrepancy.

7.4 Where a directly requested Exchange item is unavailable at the time of processing, you will be offered the option to select an alternative variant, wait for restock in accordance with our Product Availability Policy, or receive a full refund instead.

## 8. Reverse Pickup Scheduling

8.1 Where Reverse Pickup is available in your Serviceable Area, as defined in our Shipping Policy, you will be prompted to select a preferred pickup date and time window when initiating your Return request.

8.2 A Courier Partner will attempt pickup within the scheduled window. If the assigned pickup agent is unable to collect the Product due to your unavailability, you may reschedule the pickup through "My Orders," subject to a maximum of 3 reschedule attempts, after which the Return request may be automatically cancelled and require reinitiation.

8.3 At the time of Reverse Pickup, please ensure the Product is packaged securely, ideally in its original packaging, and that all conditions described in Section 3.2 are met, where applicable, as the pickup agent or subsequent quality inspection may reject Products that are inadequately packaged or do not meet eligibility conditions.

8.4 Where Reverse Pickup is not available in your area, you will be provided with a prepaid shipping label or reimbursement instructions, as described in Section 6.3, for self-shipping the Product to the designated return address.

## 9. Quality Check and Processing

9.1 Upon receipt of a returned Product at the designated fulfillment center, ShopSphere or the relevant Marketplace Seller will conduct a quality check to confirm the Product meets the applicable Return eligibility conditions.

9.2 Quality checks are generally completed within 3 to 5 business days of receipt. Upon successful completion, your Return will be approved, and the applicable Exchange, Replacement, or refund will be processed in accordance with Section 7 of this Policy or our Refund Policy, as applicable.

9.3 If a Return fails the quality check due to non-compliance with Section 3.2 (such as signs of use, missing accessories, or damaged packaging not attributable to transit), you will be notified of the rejection reason, and the Product will be returned to you, generally at no additional cost for the first such occurrence, though ShopSphere reserves the right to charge return shipping for repeated non-compliant Return attempts.

## 10. Return Timelines Summary

10.1 The following table summarizes standard timelines applicable to the Return process; specific timelines for a given Product or Order may vary and will be reflected in "My Orders":

| Stage | Typical Timeline |
|---|---|
| Return request submission | Immediate confirmation |
| Reverse Pickup scheduling | Within 2–5 business days of request |
| Transit to fulfillment center | 2–7 business days after pickup |
| Quality check | 3–5 business days after receipt |
| Refund or Exchange initiation | Within 1–2 business days of quality check approval |
| Refund credited to account (see Refund Policy) | 7–10 business days after initiation, subject to bank processing times |

## 11. Marketplace Seller Returns

11.1 Where a Product is sold by a Marketplace Seller, Return requests are subject to this Policy as a baseline standard; however, individual Marketplace Sellers may offer more generous Return terms (such as a longer Return Window), which will be disclosed on the applicable Product listing page and shall apply in place of the general terms of this Policy to the extent more favorable to you.

11.2 ShopSphere facilitates the Return process for Marketplace Seller Products, including Reverse Pickup coordination and refund processing, on behalf of Marketplace Sellers, consistent with Section 10.3 of our Terms & Conditions describing ShopSphere's role as an intermediary platform.

## 12. Relationship to Refunds, Warranty, and Cancellations

12.1 Where a Return is approved, the applicable refund shall be processed in accordance with our Refund Policy, including refund modes and timelines described therein.

12.2 Returns due to manufacturing defects discovered after the Return Window has expired may still be eligible for remedy under the applicable Warranty Policy and manufacturer warranty terms, rather than this Return Policy. Please refer to our Warranty Policy for further details.

12.3 If you wish to cancel an Order rather than return a delivered Product, please refer to our Cancellation Policy, which governs cancellations made prior to or shortly after Dispatch.

## 13. Frequently Encountered Edge Cases

13.1 **Wrong Item Received:** Treated as a seller error under Section 4; free Reverse Pickup and full refund, Replacement, or Exchange will be offered, and no restocking fee applies.

13.2 **Missing Accessories or Parts:** If a Product arrives without accessories listed in the Product description (such as a charger or manual), report this within 48 hours of delivery to support@shopsphere.com; ShopSphere will arrange shipment of the missing component or process a Return under Section 4, at your election.

13.3 **Return Window Expired but Defect Discovered Later:** Refer to Section 12.2; such issues are generally addressed under the Warranty Policy rather than this Return Policy, subject to the applicable manufacturer or ShopSphere warranty terms.

13.4 **Product Used Beyond Reasonable Inspection:** Returns may be declined under Section 9.3 if the Product shows clear signs of use beyond what is necessary to assess suitability, such as visible wear, odors, or alterations.

13.5 **Bundled or Combo Products:** Where a Product is purchased as part of a bundle or combo offer, all components of the bundle must generally be returned together to receive a full refund; returning only part of a bundle may result in a prorated refund or rejection of the Return, as disclosed on the relevant Product listing page.

13.6 **Gift Returns:** Products received as gifts may be returned by the recipient using the Order details provided by the gifter, or through a dedicated gift return flow where available; refunds for gift Returns are issued to the original payment method used for purchase, consistent with our Refund Policy, unless store credit is elected where offered.

13.7 **International Return Shipping:** For Orders delivered internationally, as described in Section 8 of our Shipping Policy, Return shipping arrangements and any applicable customs or duty implications will be communicated at the time the Return request is initiated, and reimbursement of return shipping costs is subject to Section 6.3 of this Policy.

13.8 **Partial Return of Multi-Item Order:** Where an Order contains multiple distinct Products, you may return individual Products independently; this does not affect your rights with respect to the remaining Products in the Order, consistent with Section 7.4 of our Shipping Policy regarding partial Shipments.
