# ShopSphere Refund Policy

**Effective Date: July 23, 2026**
**Last Updated: July 23, 2026**

## 1. Introduction

1.1 This Refund Policy describes how ShopSphere Commerce Pvt. Ltd. and its affiliates (collectively, "ShopSphere," "we," "us," or "our") process refunds for Orders placed through www.shopsphere.com and our mobile applications (collectively, the "Platform"), including applicable refund modes, timelines, and eligibility circumstances.

1.2 This Policy should be read together with our Terms & Conditions (particularly Section 7, Pricing and Payment), Shipping Policy, Return Policy, Cancellation Policy, and Warranty Policy, all of which are available on the Platform and incorporated by reference.

## 2. Definitions

2.1 "Refund" means the reimbursement of an amount paid by a User for a Product, shipping charge, or other fee, arising from Order cancellation, Return, non-delivery, or other circumstance described in this Policy.

2.2 "Original Payment Method" means the payment instrument used by the User to complete the transaction giving rise to the Refund, such as a credit card, debit card, net banking account, digital wallet, or UPI identifier.

2.3 "Store Credit" means a non-cash credit balance issued to a User's Account, redeemable for future purchases on the Platform.

2.4 "Refund Initiation Date" means the date on which ShopSphere or the relevant Marketplace Seller processes the Refund request and instructs the payment processor to release funds.

2.5 Capitalized terms not otherwise defined in this Policy have the meanings given to them in our Terms & Conditions, Return Policy, and Cancellation Policy.

## 3. Refund Eligibility

3.1 You may be eligible for a Refund in the following circumstances:

  3.1.1 Cancellation of an Order prior to Dispatch, in accordance with our Cancellation Policy;

  3.1.2 Cancellation of an Order by ShopSphere or a Marketplace Seller due to Product unavailability, pricing errors, or other reasons described in Section 6.2 of our Terms & Conditions and our Product Availability Policy;

  3.1.3 Approved Return of a delivered Product, in accordance with our Return Policy;

  3.1.4 Non-delivery, lost, or significantly delayed Shipments, in accordance with Sections 9 and 12 of our Shipping Policy;

  3.1.5 Failed delivery attempts resulting in Order cancellation, in accordance with Section 10.3 of our Shipping Policy;

  3.1.6 Overcharges or duplicate payment charges due to technical or payment processing errors;

  3.1.7 Warranty claims resulting in a refund remedy rather than repair or Replacement, in accordance with our Warranty Policy.

## 4. Refund Amount Calculation

4.1 **Full Product Refund.** Where an Order is cancelled prior to Dispatch, or a Return is approved due to a defect, damage, or seller error under Section 4 of our Return Policy, you will receive a full refund of the Product price, applicable Taxes, and any shipping charges paid for that Product.

4.2 **Partial Refund for Preference-Based Returns.** Where a Return is initiated for reasons of preference rather than defect (as described in Section 3 of our Return Policy), the refund will include the Product price and applicable Taxes, but original outbound shipping charges are non-refundable, consistent with Section 5.4 of our Shipping Policy, unless the Order qualified for free shipping, in which case no shipping deduction applies.

4.3 **Restocking Fees.** ShopSphere does not generally charge restocking fees for eligible Returns. Where a restocking fee applies to a specific Product category (such as large appliances or furniture), this will be clearly disclosed on the Product listing page prior to purchase and deducted from the Refund amount.

4.4 **Bundled Promotions and Discounts.** Where a Product was purchased as part of a bundle, combo, or promotional discount contingent on purchasing multiple items together, the Refund amount for a partial Return will be recalculated based on the applicable price of the retained item(s) at standard (non-bundled) pricing, which may result in a reduced Refund amount for the returned item, as disclosed at the time of Return initiation.

4.5 **Coupon and Promotional Code Adjustments.** If a coupon or promotional code was applied to an Order and the Return or cancellation of one or more items causes the Order to no longer meet the coupon's eligibility criteria (such as a minimum purchase threshold), the value of the coupon discount attributable to the returned item(s) may be deducted from the Refund amount.

4.6 **Gift Wrap and Additional Service Charges.** Optional service charges, such as gift wrapping, are non-refundable unless the Return arises from a defect, damage, or seller error under Section 4 of our Return Policy.

## 5. Refund Modes

5.1 Refunds are generally processed to the Original Payment Method used for the transaction, in accordance with applicable payment network rules.

5.2 **Card Payments.** Refunds for payments made via credit or debit card are credited back to the same card, and may take additional time to reflect in your statement depending on your card issuer's processing cycle, typically 5–7 business days after the Refund Initiation Date.

5.3 **Net Banking and UPI.** Refunds for payments made via net banking or UPI are credited to the originating bank account or UPI-linked account, typically within 3–5 business days after the Refund Initiation Date.

5.4 **Digital Wallets.** Refunds for payments made via digital wallets are credited back to the same wallet, typically within 1–3 business days after the Refund Initiation Date.

5.5 **Cash on Delivery (COD) Orders.** For Orders paid via cash on delivery, refunds are processed via bank transfer to a bank account provided by you, or as Store Credit at your election, as ShopSphere is unable to return cash directly. Bank transfer details must be submitted through "My Orders" and are typically processed within 7–10 business days of submission.

5.6 **Store Credit.** You may elect to receive a Refund as Store Credit instead of a refund to your Original Payment Method, which is typically credited to your Account within 24 hours of Refund approval and does not expire unless otherwise stated at the time of issuance. Store Credit issued as a result of ShopSphere-initiated compensation (such as goodwill gestures) may be subject to a stated expiration period, which will be disclosed at the time of issuance.

5.7 **Gift Card Purchases.** Where an Order was paid for, in whole or in part, using a ShopSphere gift card, the corresponding portion of any Refund will be credited back to a gift card or as Store Credit, and only the remaining balance, if any, paid via another payment method will be refunded to that Original Payment Method.

5.8 Where the Original Payment Method is no longer valid or accessible (such as an expired card or closed bank account), ShopSphere will contact you to arrange an alternative refund mode, which may include Store Credit or bank transfer, subject to identity verification.

## 6. Refund Timelines

6.1 Refunds are initiated promptly following approval of the underlying cancellation, Return, or claim:

  6.1.1 For Order cancellations prior to Dispatch, Refunds are typically initiated within 1–2 business days of the cancellation request;

  6.1.2 For approved Returns, Refunds are typically initiated within 1–2 business days following successful quality check, as described in Section 9 of our Return Policy;

  6.1.3 For non-delivery, lost package, or significant delay claims, Refunds are typically initiated within 2–5 business days following completion of the Courier Partner investigation described in Sections 9 and 12 of our Shipping Policy.

6.2 Following Refund initiation, the time for funds to reflect in your account varies by refund mode, as described in Section 5 above, and is also subject to processing times of your bank, card issuer, or payment provider, which are beyond ShopSphere's control.

6.3 You may track the status of your Refund via "My Orders" in your Account, which will display the Refund Initiation Date and expected credit timeline based on your Original Payment Method.

6.4 If you have not received your Refund within 15 business days of the Refund Initiation Date (or the applicable timeline described in Section 5, whichever is longer), please contact customer support at support@shopsphere.com with your Order number and Refund reference for further investigation.

## 7. Refunds for Marketplace Seller Orders

7.1 For Products sold by Marketplace Sellers, ShopSphere facilitates Refund processing on behalf of the Marketplace Seller, consistent with Section 10.3 of our Terms & Conditions. Refund amounts and timelines described in this Policy apply uniformly regardless of whether the Product was sold by ShopSphere directly or by a Marketplace Seller.

7.2 Where a Marketplace Seller disputes a Refund claim (for example, disputing that a Returned Product was defective), ShopSphere will independently review the claim, including any photographic or video evidence provided, and make a final determination regarding Refund eligibility in order to ensure consistent treatment of Users, consistent with our role as an intermediary platform.

## 8. Failed or Delayed Refund Transactions

8.1 If a Refund transaction fails due to invalid or outdated payment details, ShopSphere will notify you and request updated information to complete the Refund, which may include an alternative payment method or bank account for direct transfer.

8.2 If a Refund is delayed due to a technical error on ShopSphere's payment processing systems, ShopSphere will make reasonable efforts to resolve the issue and complete the Refund within 5 additional business days of identifying the error, and will proactively notify affected Users where reasonably practicable.

## 9. Refunds Involving Disputed or Chargeback Transactions

9.1 If you initiate a chargeback or payment dispute directly with your card issuer or bank for a transaction that is also subject to an active Return or Refund request on the Platform, ShopSphere reserves the right to pause processing of the Platform-based Refund pending resolution of the chargeback, to avoid duplicate reimbursement.

9.2 ShopSphere may contest chargebacks that it believes are unwarranted, including by submitting evidence of delivery, Return eligibility determinations, or prior Refund issuance, to the relevant payment processor or card network.

## 10. Non-Refundable Charges

10.1 The following charges are generally non-refundable, except where the underlying Return or cancellation arises from a defect, damage, or seller error, or where otherwise stated in this Policy or the applicable Product listing page:

  10.1.1 Original outbound shipping charges for preference-based Returns, as described in Section 4.2;

  10.1.2 Gift wrap and other optional service charges, as described in Section 4.6;

  10.1.3 Charges for Non-Returnable Products, as defined in Section 5 of our Return Policy, except where the Return arises under Section 4 of that Policy;

  10.1.4 Currency conversion fees or charges levied by your bank or payment provider on international transactions.

## 11. Relationship to Return, Cancellation, and Warranty Policies

11.1 This Refund Policy governs the mechanics of Refund processing (amount, mode, and timeline). Eligibility for a Refund in the first instance is governed by our Return Policy (for delivered Products), our Cancellation Policy (for Orders cancelled before or shortly after Dispatch), and our Warranty Policy (for post-Return-Window defect claims resolved via refund rather than repair).

## 12. Frequently Encountered Edge Cases

12.1 **Refund to an Expired or Closed Card:** Refer to Section 5.8; ShopSphere will contact you to arrange an alternative refund mode.

12.2 **Partial Order Refund Due to Split Shipment Issues:** Where only part of a multi-item Order is cancelled, returned, or affected by a delivery issue, the Refund will be calculated only for the affected Product(s), consistent with Section 7.4 of our Shipping Policy and Section 13.8 of our Return Policy.

12.3 **Refund Reflects Different Amount Than Expected:** This is typically due to deductions described in Sections 4.2–4.6 (non-refundable shipping, coupon adjustments, or bundle repricing); the itemized Refund breakdown is available via "My Orders."

12.4 **Duplicate Charge for Single Order:** Contact support@shopsphere.com immediately with your Order number and payment reference; duplicate charges confirmed by our payment processor will be refunded in full within 5 business days of confirmation.

12.5 **Refund Requested After Store Credit Already Issued:** Once Store Credit has been issued and used for a subsequent purchase, the original Store Credit cannot be reversed to a Refund on the Original Payment Method; Users should contact support@shopsphere.com before using issued Store Credit if a Refund to the Original Payment Method is preferred and eligible.

12.6 **International Refunds and Currency Fluctuation:** For international Orders, the Refund amount will reflect the original transaction currency and amount; ShopSphere is not responsible for differences arising from currency exchange rate fluctuations between the purchase and Refund dates, consistent with Section 8.6 of our Shipping Policy.
