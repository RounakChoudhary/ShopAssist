# ShopSphere Cancellation Policy

**Effective Date: July 23, 2026**
**Last Updated: July 23, 2026**

## 1. Introduction

1.1 This Cancellation Policy describes the circumstances and procedures under which Users may cancel Orders placed through www.shopsphere.com and our mobile applications (collectively, the "Platform"), whether before or after Dispatch.

1.2 This Policy should be read together with our Terms & Conditions (particularly Sections 6 and 7), Shipping Policy, Return Policy, Refund Policy, and Warranty Policy, all of which are available on the Platform and incorporated by reference.

## 2. Definitions

2.1 "Cancellation" means the termination of an Order, in whole or in part, prior to completion of delivery, whether initiated by the User, ShopSphere, or a Marketplace Seller.

2.2 "Order Lock Period" means the brief window immediately following Order placement during which the Order may be freely modified or cancelled before it enters processing.

2.3 "Dispatch" has the meaning given in our Shipping Policy, referring to the point at which a Shipment is handed over to a Courier Partner.

2.4 Capitalized terms not otherwise defined in this Policy have the meanings given to them in our Terms & Conditions, Shipping Policy, and Refund Policy.

## 3. Cancellation Before Dispatch

3.1 **Order Lock Period.** Upon placing an Order, you have a brief Order Lock Period, typically up to 30 minutes, during which you may cancel or modify the Order directly through "My Orders" without any restriction.

3.2 **Cancellation During Processing.** Following the Order Lock Period and prior to Dispatch, you may still cancel your Order, in whole or in part (for multi-item Orders), by accessing "My Orders" and selecting the "Cancel" option for the relevant Product(s), consistent with Section 6.3 of our Terms & Conditions.

3.3 Cancellations made prior to Dispatch are processed immediately, and no shipping charges will be levied for the cancelled Product(s). Any amount already charged will be refunded in full in accordance with our Refund Policy.

3.4 For Products with expedited processing timelines (such as same-day dispatch categories), the window for free cancellation prior to Dispatch may be shorter, as disclosed on the Product listing page at the time of purchase.

## 4. Cancellation After Dispatch

4.1 Once a Product has been dispatched and handed over to a Courier Partner, it generally cannot be cancelled through the standard Order cancellation flow. Instead, you have the following options:

  4.1.1 **Refuse Delivery:** You may refuse to accept the Shipment upon attempted delivery. Refused Shipments will be returned to the originating fulfillment center, and upon confirmation of the Product's condition, a refund will be processed in accordance with Section 5 below and our Refund Policy;

  4.1.2 **Return After Delivery:** If you have already accepted delivery, you may initiate a Return in accordance with our Return Policy, subject to the applicable Return Window and eligibility conditions described therein.

4.2 Where a Product is in transit and cannot be recalled, ShopSphere may, at its discretion and where technically feasible through the Courier Partner, attempt to intercept and reroute the Shipment back to the fulfillment center upon your cancellation request; however, this is not guaranteed and is subject to Courier Partner capabilities and the Shipment's current transit stage.

4.3 For Pre-Order and Backorder Products, as defined in our Product Availability Policy, cancellation after Dispatch but before delivery follows the same procedure described in Section 4.1.

## 5. Refunds for Post-Dispatch Cancellations

5.1 Where a Shipment is refused at delivery or returned following interception under Section 4.2, and the Product is confirmed to be in its original, unused condition upon return to the fulfillment center, you will receive a full refund of the Product price and applicable Taxes.

5.2 Original outbound shipping charges for post-Dispatch cancellations via refusal are treated in the same manner as preference-based Returns under Section 4.2 of our Refund Policy, meaning such charges are generally non-refundable unless the cancellation arises from a defect, damage, delay, or seller error, in which case Section 4.1 of our Refund Policy applies.

5.3 Refund timelines and modes for post-Dispatch cancellations follow the same procedures described in Sections 5 and 6 of our Refund Policy.

## 6. Cancellation by ShopSphere or Marketplace Sellers

6.1 Consistent with Section 6.2 of our Terms & Conditions, ShopSphere or the relevant Marketplace Seller may cancel an Order, in whole or in part, prior to Dispatch, for reasons including:

  6.1.1 Product unavailability or inventory discrepancies, as described in Section 4 of our Product Availability Policy;

  6.1.2 Pricing or listing errors, as described in Section 7.2 of our Terms & Conditions and Section 10 of our Product Availability Policy;

  6.1.3 Suspected fraudulent activity or payment failure;

  6.1.4 Inability to deliver to the specified address due to jurisdictional, regulatory, or Serviceable Area restrictions, as described in Section 11 of our Product Availability Policy and Section 6.6 of our Shipping Policy;

  6.1.5 Exceeding applicable purchase quantity limits, as described in Section 8.2 of our Product Availability Policy.

6.2 In such cases, you will be notified promptly of the cancellation and the reason therefor, and any amount charged will be refunded in full within the timelines described in our Refund Policy. No cancellation fee will be charged to you in such circumstances.

## 7. Cancellation of Multi-Item and Multi-Seller Orders

7.1 For Orders containing multiple Products, whether from ShopSphere or multiple Marketplace Sellers, you may cancel individual line items independently, and cancellation of one item does not affect the status of other items in the same Order, consistent with Section 6.4 of our Terms & Conditions regarding multi-seller Orders.

7.2 Where cancellation of an item causes the remaining Order to fall below a threshold required for a promotional benefit (such as free shipping or a bundle discount), the pricing and shipping charges for the remaining item(s) will be recalculated accordingly, consistent with Section 4.4 and 4.5 of our Refund Policy.

## 8. Cancellation of Pre-Orders and Backorders

8.1 Pre-Orders and Backorders, as defined in our Product Availability Policy, may be cancelled at any time prior to Dispatch without restriction or charge, as described in Sections 5.3 and 6.3 of our Product Availability Policy.

8.2 If ShopSphere or the relevant Marketplace Seller delays a Pre-Order or Backorder beyond the thresholds described in Sections 5.4 and 6.3 of our Product Availability Policy, you will be proactively offered the option to cancel for a full refund.

## 9. Subscription and Recurring Order Cancellations

9.1 For Products or Services purchased through a subscription or recurring delivery arrangement (such as periodic replenishment subscriptions), you may cancel future scheduled deliveries at any time through "My Subscriptions" in your Account, provided cancellation is submitted at least 48 hours prior to the next scheduled processing date.

9.2 Cancellation of a subscription does not affect Orders that have already been processed or dispatched prior to the cancellation request, which are governed by the standard provisions of this Policy applicable to individual Orders.

9.3 No cancellation fee applies to subscription cancellations, and any prepaid amounts for unprocessed future deliveries will be refunded in accordance with our Refund Policy.

## 10. Cancellation Fees and Exceptions

10.1 ShopSphere does not generally charge cancellation fees for Orders cancelled prior to Dispatch, consistent with Section 3.3.

10.2 For certain custom, personalized, or made-to-order Products, cancellation may not be possible once production or customization has commenced, as disclosed on the applicable Product listing page at the time of purchase. Where cancellation of such Products is permitted prior to commencement of customization, it will follow the standard procedure in Section 3.

10.3 For Services booked with a specific date or time slot (such as installation or assembly services bundled with a Product purchase), cancellation within 24 hours of the scheduled slot may be subject to a rescheduling or cancellation fee, as disclosed at the time of booking.

## 11. Frequently Encountered Edge Cases

11.1 **Cancellation Request Submitted Just Before Dispatch:** If your cancellation request is submitted but the Product is dispatched before the request can be processed, the Order will follow the post-Dispatch cancellation process described in Section 4, including refusal of delivery or Return after delivery.

11.2 **Partial Cancellation of a Bundled Offer:** As described in Section 7.2, cancelling one item from a bundle may affect pricing of retained items; the recalculated total will be displayed before you confirm the partial cancellation.

11.3 **Cancellation After Payment but Before Order Confirmation:** In rare cases where payment is captured but Order confirmation is delayed or fails, consistent with Section 6.1 of our Terms & Conditions, ShopSphere will automatically cancel the Order and issue a full refund within the timelines described in our Refund Policy without requiring a manual cancellation request.

11.4 **Cancellation of Gift Orders:** Orders marked for gift delivery may be cancelled by the purchasing User (not the recipient) prior to Dispatch through the standard flow in Section 3; once dispatched, the recipient may refuse delivery or initiate a Return under Section 4.1, subject to the purchasing User being notified of any resulting refund per Section 6.4 of our Return Policy regarding gift returns.

11.5 **Repeated Cancellations by a Single User:** ShopSphere reserves the right to review Accounts exhibiting a pattern of frequent Order placements and cancellations that suggest misuse of the Platform's inventory reservation or promotional systems, and may restrict cancellation privileges or Account access in accordance with Section 4.5 of our Terms & Conditions.

11.6 **Cancellation Due to Payment Method Decline After Dispatch:** If a payment issue is discovered after Dispatch (such as a reversed or disputed charge), ShopSphere reserves the right to redirect the Shipment back to the fulfillment center where feasible or to require resolution of the payment issue before completing delivery.
