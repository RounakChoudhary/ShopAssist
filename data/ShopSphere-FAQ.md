# ShopSphere Help Center: Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

**Effective Date: July 23, 2026**
**Last Updated: July 23, 2026**

## Introduction

This FAQ answers common questions about using ShopSphere, organized by category. It is intended as a quick-reference guide and should be read alongside our full policies: Terms & Conditions, Privacy Policy, Cookie Policy, Product Availability Policy, Shipping Policy, Return Policy, Refund Policy, Cancellation Policy, and Warranty Policy, all available on the Platform. For issues not addressed here, please see our Contact Us page or Grievance Policy.

---

## 1. Account

**1.1 How do I create a ShopSphere account?**
Click "Sign Up" on the homepage or app, and provide your email address or phone number, along with a password. You can also register using a social media login where available. See Section 4 of our Terms & Conditions for account eligibility requirements.

**1.2 Can I use ShopSphere without creating an account?**
Yes, guest checkout is available for most purchases. However, creating an account lets you track Orders, save addresses, and access faster checkout and Return/Refund processing.

**1.3 I forgot my password. How do I reset it?**
Click "Forgot Password" on the login page and follow the instructions sent to your registered email or phone number to reset your password securely.

**1.4 Can I change my registered email address or phone number?**
Yes, go to Account Settings and update your contact details. You may need to verify the new email or phone number via OTP.

**1.5 How do I delete my ShopSphere account?**
Go to Account Settings and select "Deactivate Account," or contact support@shopsphere.com. Deactivation is subject to completion of pending Orders, disputes, or payment obligations, as described in Section 4.6 of our Terms & Conditions.

**1.6 Can I have more than one ShopSphere account?**
No, our Terms & Conditions permit only one active account per User unless otherwise authorized in writing. Duplicate accounts may be merged or suspended.

**1.7 Why was my account suspended?**
Accounts may be suspended for reasons including suspected fraud, Terms violations, or unusual activity, as described in Section 4.5 of our Terms & Conditions. You will generally be notified of the reason via email.

**1.8 How do I update my saved addresses?**
Go to Account Settings > Address Book to add, edit, or remove saved delivery addresses.

**1.9 Is there a minimum age to create an account?**
Yes, you must be at least 18, or use the Platform under parental supervision if you are between 13 and 18, as described in Section 3 of our Terms & Conditions and Section 12 of our Privacy Policy.

**1.10 Can I merge two accounts I accidentally created?**
Contact support@shopsphere.com with both registered email addresses, and we will assist in merging Order history where technically feasible.

## 2. Orders

**2.1 How do I place an Order?**
Add items to your cart, proceed to checkout, select a delivery address and payment method, and confirm your Order. You'll receive a confirmation email once the Order is accepted.

**2.2 Can I modify my Order after placing it?**
You can modify quantities or addresses prior to Dispatch through "My Orders." Once dispatched, modifications are generally not possible; see our Shipping Policy Section 11 and Cancellation Policy Section 4.

**2.3 How do I track my Order?**
Go to "My Orders" and select the relevant Order to view real-time tracking information and Courier Partner details, as described in our Shipping Policy.

**2.4 Why was my Order cancelled by ShopSphere?**
Orders may be cancelled due to stock unavailability, pricing errors, suspected fraud, or delivery restrictions, as described in Section 6.2 of our Terms & Conditions and Section 6 of our Cancellation Policy. You will receive a full refund for any cancelled item.

**2.5 Can I place an Order for delivery to someone else as a gift?**
Yes, select the gift delivery option at checkout and provide the recipient's address. See Section 13.6 of our Return Policy for gift return handling.

**2.6 What happens if a Product in my Order is out of stock after I order it?**
You will be notified, and the affected item will be cancelled and refunded, or a substitute offered, as described in Section 4.2 of our Product Availability Policy.

**2.7 Can I combine multiple Orders into a single Shipment?**
Generally, no. Each Order is processed and shipped independently once confirmed, even if placed within a short time of each other.

**2.8 Why did my Order arrive in multiple packages?**
This is a partial Shipment, common for multi-item or multi-seller Orders, as described in Section 7 of our Shipping Policy. It is not an error and does not incur extra shipping charges.

**2.9 How do I view my past Order history?**
Go to "My Orders" and filter by date range or status to view historical Orders, invoices, and Return/Refund status.

**2.10 Can I reorder a previous purchase easily?**
Yes, select "Buy Again" next to any past Order item in "My Orders," subject to current availability and pricing.

**2.11 What does "Order Confirmed" versus "Order Placed" mean?**
"Order Placed" means we've received your request; "Order Confirmed" means it has passed fraud and inventory checks and is being processed, per Section 6.1 of our Terms & Conditions.

**2.12 Can I get an invoice for my Order?**
Yes, tax invoices are available in "My Orders" for download, consistent with Section 8.4 of our Terms & Conditions.

## 3. Shipping

**3.1 How long does delivery take?**
Delivery timelines vary by location, typically 2–4 business days in metro areas and up to 10 business days in remote areas; see Section 4 of our Shipping Policy for details.

**3.2 Is shipping free?**
Free shipping is available on Orders meeting a minimum value threshold or through membership programs, as described in Section 5.2 of our Shipping Policy; otherwise, charges are calculated at checkout.

**3.3 Which courier partners does ShopSphere use?**
We work with a network of national and regional Courier Partners, and in some cases our own delivery fleet, as described in Section 6 of our Shipping Policy. The assigned partner is shown in your tracking details.

**3.4 Do you ship internationally?**
Yes, to select countries, subject to Product eligibility and customs regulations, as described in Section 8 of our Shipping Policy.

**3.5 Who pays customs duties for international orders?**
The recipient, as the importer of record, is responsible for any customs duties or import taxes, per Section 8.3 of our Shipping Policy.

**3.6 My package shows no tracking movement. What should I do?**
If there's no movement for more than 3 business days, contact support@shopsphere.com; we will investigate with the Courier Partner as described in Section 9 of our Shipping Policy.

**3.7 What if I miss my delivery?**
Courier Partners typically attempt delivery up to 3 times; you'll be notified of failed attempts and can reschedule, per Section 10 of our Shipping Policy.

**3.8 Can I change my delivery address after placing an order?**
Only before Dispatch, by contacting support@shopsphere.com; after Dispatch, address changes are generally not possible, per Section 11.2 of our Shipping Policy.

**3.9 What happens if I entered the wrong address?**
Contact support immediately; if the package is already dispatched, see Section 11.4 of our Shipping Policy regarding redelivery or refund options.

**3.10 What if my package is lost in transit?**
Report it to support@shopsphere.com; we investigate and offer a replacement or full refund per Section 12 of our Shipping Policy.

**3.11 What if my package arrives damaged?**
Report within 48 hours with photos to support@shopsphere.com for a free replacement or refund, per Section 13 of our Shipping Policy.

**3.12 What is Open-Box Delivery?**
For select high-value products, you may inspect the item with the delivery agent present before accepting it, per Section 15 of our Shipping Policy.

**3.13 Can I schedule a specific delivery time?**
Some areas support delivery time-slot selection at checkout; availability varies by location and Courier Partner capacity.

**3.14 Do you deliver on weekends and holidays?**
Delivery availability on Sundays and public holidays varies by region and Courier Partner; standard timelines exclude these days unless stated otherwise.

## 4. Payments

**4.1 What payment methods are accepted?**
Credit/debit cards, net banking, UPI, digital wallets, buy-now-pay-later services, gift cards, and cash-on-delivery where available, per Section 7.3 of our Terms & Conditions.

**4.2 Is it safe to save my card details on ShopSphere?**
Yes, card data is handled by PCI-DSS compliant payment processors; ShopSphere does not store full card numbers on its own servers, per Section 10.4 of our Terms & Conditions.

**4.3 Why was my payment declined?**
This may be due to bank restrictions, insufficient funds, incorrect card details, or fraud-prevention checks; contact your bank or try an alternate payment method.

**4.4 I was charged twice for one order. What do I do?**
Contact payments@shopsphere.com with your Order number; confirmed duplicate charges are refunded within 5 business days per Section 12.4 of our Refund Policy.

**4.5 Can I pay in installments?**
Where available, buy-now-pay-later or EMI options are shown at checkout for eligible Orders and payment methods.

**4.6 Is Cash on Delivery available everywhere?**
COD availability depends on your Serviceable Area and Order value; eligibility is shown at checkout.

**4.7 How do refunds for COD orders work?**
Refunds for COD Orders are processed via bank transfer or Store Credit, since cash cannot be returned directly; see Section 5.5 of our Refund Policy.

**4.8 Can I use multiple payment methods for one order?**
In some cases, such as combining a gift card with another payment method, this is supported; check available options at checkout.

**4.9 Do you charge extra for international currency conversion?**
ShopSphere does not charge conversion fees, but your bank or card issuer may apply their own fees, per Section 8.6 of our Shipping Policy.

**4.10 What should I do if my UPI payment is deducted but the order isn't confirmed?**
Contact payments@shopsphere.com with the transaction reference; failed order confirmations with captured payment are automatically refunded per Section 11.3 of our Cancellation Policy.

## 5. Returns

**5.1 What is the return window?**
Typically 7–15 days from delivery, depending on the Product, as shown on the Product listing page and Section 3.1 of our Return Policy.

**5.2 How do I initiate a return?**
Go to "My Orders," select the item, choose "Return," and specify a reason, per Section 6 of our Return Policy.

**5.3 What products cannot be returned?**
Items like innerwear, opened cosmetics, customized products, and digital goods are generally non-returnable except for defects; see Section 5 of our Return Policy.

**5.4 Do I need original packaging to return a product?**
Yes, generally, along with tags, accessories, and manuals, unless the return is due to a defect or seller error, per Section 3.2 of our Return Policy.

**5.5 How is a reverse pickup scheduled?**
You'll select a pickup date/time when initiating your return; a Courier Partner will collect it, per Section 8 of our Return Policy.

**5.6 What if reverse pickup isn't available in my area?**
You'll receive self-shipping instructions and reimbursement for reasonable shipping costs where applicable, per Section 6.3 of our Return Policy.

**5.7 Can I exchange an item instead of returning it?**
Yes, where available, select "Exchange" for a different size, color, or variant, per Section 7 of our Return Policy.

**5.8 What if my returned item fails quality inspection?**
It will be sent back to you, generally at no cost for a first occurrence, per Section 9.3 of our Return Policy.

**5.9 Can I return part of a multi-item order?**
Yes, you can return individual items independently; this doesn't affect the rest of your Order, per Section 13.8 of our Return Policy.

**5.10 What if I received the wrong item?**
This is treated as a seller error; free pickup and full refund, replacement, or exchange applies, per Section 4 of our Return Policy.

**5.11 Can I return a gift I received?**
Yes, using the Order details from the purchaser or through a dedicated gift return flow, per Section 13.6 of our Return Policy.

**5.12 What if the return window has already expired?**
If a defect is discovered after the window closes, it may be covered under our Warranty Policy instead; see Section 12.2 of our Return Policy.

## 6. Refunds

**6.1 How long does a refund take?**
Typically 7–10 business days after initiation, though this varies by payment method; see Section 6 of our Refund Policy.

**6.2 How will I receive my refund?**
Generally to your original payment method; COD orders are refunded via bank transfer or Store Credit, per Section 5 of our Refund Policy.

**6.3 Why is my refund amount less than what I paid?**
Deductions may include non-refundable shipping charges, coupon adjustments, or bundle repricing, per Section 4 of our Refund Policy.

**6.4 Can I choose to receive Store Credit instead of a refund to my card?**
Yes, Store Credit is typically credited within 24 hours and does not expire unless stated otherwise, per Section 5.6 of our Refund Policy.

**6.5 I haven't received my refund after 15 days. What now?**
Contact support@shopsphere.com with your Order number and refund reference for investigation, per Section 6.4 of our Refund Policy.

**6.6 Are shipping charges refunded?**
Only if the return is due to a defect, damage, delay, or seller error; otherwise original shipping charges are non-refundable, per Section 4.2 of our Refund Policy.

**6.7 What happens to my refund if I used a coupon?**
The coupon's discount value attributable to a returned item may be deducted from your refund if it affects the Order's eligibility, per Section 4.5 of our Refund Policy.

**6.8 Can a refund be sent to a different bank account or card?**
Generally no, refunds go to the original payment method; exceptions apply for COD or if the original method is no longer valid, per Section 5.8 of our Refund Policy.

**6.9 Will I be refunded if my order is delayed indefinitely?**
Yes, you may request cancellation and full refund for significantly delayed shipments, per Section 9.4 of our Shipping Policy.

## 7. Warranty

**7.1 What is covered under warranty?**
Manufacturing defects in materials or workmanship, subject to exclusions like misuse or wear and tear, per Section 8 of our Warranty Policy.

**7.2 How is warranty different from the return window?**
Returns address a variety of reasons shortly after delivery; warranty addresses defects that arise during ownership, often after the return window closes, per Section 1.3 of our Warranty Policy.

**7.3 How do I file a warranty claim?**
Go to "My Orders," select "Warranty Claim," or contact support@shopsphere.com with your Order number and defect description, per Section 6 of our Warranty Policy.

**7.4 Does ShopSphere offer extended warranty?**
Yes, for select electronics and appliances, purchasable at checkout, per Section 5 of our Warranty Policy.

**7.5 What voids a warranty?**
Unauthorized repairs, misuse, liquid damage, tampered serial numbers, and non-genuine parts, among others; see Section 8 of our Warranty Policy for the full list.

**7.6 Can I get a replacement instead of a repair?**
Yes, especially if the product has already had 2 or more repair attempts for the same issue, per Section 12.3 of our Warranty Policy.

**7.7 Do marketplace seller products carry a warranty?**
Typically the manufacturer's warranty applies rather than the seller's; ShopSphere facilitates the claim process, per Section 9 of our Warranty Policy.

**7.8 What if the manufacturer stops honoring warranties?**
ShopSphere or the Extended Warranty provider will attempt alternative remedies where a ShopSphere or Extended Warranty exists; see Section 12.6 of our Warranty Policy.

**7.9 Is warranty coverage valid internationally?**
It depends on the manufacturer's international warranty policy; some brands limit service to the country of original sale, per Section 10 of our Warranty Policy.

## 8. Coupons

**8.1 How do I apply a coupon code?**
Enter the code in the "Apply Coupon" field at checkout before completing payment.

**8.2 Why isn't my coupon code working?**
Check the expiration date, minimum purchase requirement, and eligible product categories; some coupons are user-specific or single-use.

**8.3 Can I combine multiple coupons on one order?**
Generally, only one coupon or promotional code can be applied per Order unless explicitly stated otherwise in the offer terms.

**8.4 What happens to my coupon discount if I return an item?**
The discount attributable to the returned item may be deducted from your refund, as described in Section 4.5 of our Refund Policy.

**8.5 Do coupons apply to Marketplace Seller products?**
This depends on the specific coupon's terms; some are restricted to ShopSphere-fulfilled Products only, as indicated in the offer details.

**8.6 Can I use a coupon after checkout if I forgot to apply it?**
Generally no, coupons must be applied before payment is completed; contact support for exceptions in cases of technical error.

**8.7 Why was my account flagged for coupon misuse?**
Repeated fraudulent use, stacking of restricted codes, or violation of one-per-user limits may result in review or restriction, per Section 5.3 of our Terms & Conditions.

**8.8 Do gift cards work the same way as coupons?**
No, gift cards are a stored payment balance, not a discount code; they are applied as a payment method at checkout.

## 9. Product Availability

**9.1 What does "Out of Stock" mean?**
The Product is temporarily unavailable for purchase; you may opt in for "Back in Stock" notifications, per Section 4 of our Product Availability Policy.

**9.2 What is a Backorder?**
A Product you can still order despite temporary unavailability, fulfilled once restocked, per Section 5 of our Product Availability Policy.

**9.3 What is a Pre-Order?**
A Product listed for purchase before its official release or restock date, per Section 6 of our Product Availability Policy.

**9.4 Why did my in-stock item become unavailable after I added it to cart?**
Stock levels update in real time and may be claimed by other Users; you'll be notified if this happens at checkout, per Section 3.3 of our Product Availability Policy.

**9.5 Can I get notified when an item is back in stock?**
Yes, select "Notify Me" on the Product listing page for Out of Stock items.

**9.6 Why is a product unavailable for delivery to my location?**
Some Products are restricted by region due to regulatory or licensing reasons, per Section 11 of our Product Availability Policy.

**9.7 What happens if a product I pre-ordered is delayed?**
You'll be notified, and if the delay exceeds 60 days, you can cancel for a full refund, per Section 6.3 of our Product Availability Policy.

**9.8 Why was a product I purchased later discontinued?**
Discontinuation doesn't affect your existing warranty or return rights for units already purchased, per Section 7.3 of our Product Availability Policy.

## 10. Seller Issues

**10.1 What is a Marketplace Seller?**
A third-party seller who lists and fulfills Products through the ShopSphere Platform, as defined in Section 2.4 of our Terms & Conditions.

**10.2 Who do I contact if a seller doesn't ship my order?**
Contact ShopSphere customer support; we facilitate resolution as the intermediary Platform, per Section 10.3 of our Terms & Conditions.

**10.3 How do I report a seller for selling counterfeit goods?**
Email marketplace-integrity@shopsphere.com with details and evidence; we investigate and may suspend the seller per our seller policies.

**10.4 Does ShopSphere guarantee the quality of seller products?**
ShopSphere facilitates transactions but the contract is between you and the seller; however, ShopSphere assists in Return and Refund processing as described in our Return and Refund Policies.

**10.5 Can I message a seller directly?**
Yes, through the Product page or Order details, for questions about the item, but transactional issues like returns should go through the Platform's standard flow.

**10.6 What if a seller's listed warranty turns out to be false?**
Report this to marketplace-integrity@shopsphere.com; we will investigate and facilitate an appropriate remedy, per Section 9.2 of our Warranty Policy.

**10.7 Why do different sellers offer different prices for the same product?**
Marketplace Sellers set their own pricing and shipping charges independently, disclosed at checkout, per Section 5.3 of our Shipping Policy.

## 11. Technical Problems

**11.1 The website or app is not loading. What should I do?**
Try clearing your browser cache, updating the app, or checking your internet connection; persistent issues can be reported to support@shopsphere.com.

**11.2 I can't add items to my cart. What's wrong?**
This may be a temporary glitch or stock issue; try refreshing the page or reinstalling the app.

**11.3 My OTP isn't arriving. What should I do?**
Check your network signal and spam folder; if the issue persists, request a resend or contact support@shopsphere.com.

**11.4 The app crashed during checkout. Was my order placed?**
Check "My Orders" and your payment app/bank statement; if charged but no Order appears, contact support immediately, per Section 11.3 of our Cancellation Policy.

**11.5 Why do I see different prices on the app versus website?**
Prices should be consistent, but caching or promotional timing differences can occasionally occur; refresh and check again, or contact support.

**11.6 How do I clear my browsing cache for ShopSphere?**
This can typically be done through your browser's settings under "Privacy" or "History"; instructions vary by browser.

**11.7 Can I use ShopSphere on multiple devices simultaneously?**
Yes, your Account can be logged in on multiple devices, though for security reasons some sensitive actions may require re-verification.

## 12. Security

**12.1 How does ShopSphere protect my payment information?**
Payment data is processed via PCI-DSS compliant processors and encrypted in transit using TLS, per Section 10 of our Privacy Policy.

**12.2 What should I do if I suspect unauthorized access to my account?**
Change your password immediately and contact security@shopsphere.com, per Section 4.2 of our Terms & Conditions.

**12.3 Does ShopSphere offer two-factor authentication?**
Yes, multi-factor authentication options are available in Account Settings for enhanced security, per Section 10.1.5 of our Privacy Policy.

**12.4 How do I create a strong password?**
Use a unique combination of upper and lowercase letters, numbers, and symbols, avoiding reused passwords from other sites; see our Safe Shopping Guide for more tips.

**12.5 Will ShopSphere ever ask for my full card number or OTP over phone or email?**
No, ShopSphere staff will never ask for your OTP, full card number, or password; treat such requests as fraudulent, per our Safe Shopping Guide.

**12.6 How do I report a phishing email pretending to be ShopSphere?**
Forward it to security@shopsphere.com without clicking any links, per our Safe Shopping Guide.

## 14. Fraud

**14.1 How do I recognize a fake ShopSphere website?**
Verify the URL is exactly www.shopsphere.com with a secure padlock icon; see our Safe Shopping Guide for detailed identification tips.

**14.2 What if I receive a suspicious call claiming to be from ShopSphere?**
Do not share OTPs, passwords, or card details; verify by contacting official support channels directly, per our Safe Shopping Guide.

**14.3 I was scammed by someone claiming to be a ShopSphere representative. What do I do?**
Report immediately to security@shopsphere.com and your local law enforcement; ShopSphere will investigate and assist where possible.

**14.4 How can I verify a refund SMS is genuinely from ShopSphere?**
Cross-check the refund status directly in "My Orders" rather than trusting SMS links alone.

**14.5 What if my card was charged for an order I never placed?**
Contact payments@shopsphere.com and your bank immediately to dispute the charge and secure your Account.

**14.6 Does ShopSphere compensate victims of third-party scams impersonating us?**
ShopSphere is not liable for losses caused by third-party impersonation fraud but will assist with investigation and reporting, per Section 14.3 of our Terms & Conditions regarding indemnification and general liability limitations.
