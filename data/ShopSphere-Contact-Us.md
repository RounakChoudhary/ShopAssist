# ShopSphere Contact Us

**Effective Date: July 23, 2026**
**Last Updated: July 23, 2026**

## 1. Introduction

1.1 This Contact Us page describes the support channels available to ShopSphere Users, expected response times, business hours, and the escalation matrix for unresolved issues. It should be read alongside our Grievance Policy, which governs the formal complaint resolution process, and our FAQ, which addresses common questions.

## 2. Support Channels Overview

2.1 ShopSphere offers multiple channels through which you can reach our support team, summarized below and described in further detail in the sections that follow:

| Channel | Best For | Availability |
|---|---|---|
| Live Chat | Quick queries, order status, urgent issues | 24/7 |
| Email Support | Detailed queries, documentation-heavy issues, complaints | 24/7 (responses during business hours) |
| Phone Support | Urgent issues, complex troubleshooting | Daily, 8:00 AM – 10:00 PM IST |
| Help Center / FAQ | Self-service for common questions | 24/7 |
| Social Media | General queries, public visibility issues | Business hours, monitored extended hours |
| Grievance Officer | Unresolved complaints, regulatory matters | Business days, per our Grievance Policy |

## 3. Email Support

3.1 Email support is available for all types of inquiries and is recommended for issues requiring documentation, such as photographs of damaged items or detailed Order disputes.

3.2 **General Support:** support@shopsphere.com — for Order status, shipping, returns, refunds, and general inquiries.

3.3 **Payments and Refunds Desk:** payments@shopsphere.com — for payment failures, duplicate charges, and refund-specific queries, consistent with our Refund Policy.

3.4 **Warranty Claims Desk:** warranty@shopsphere.com — for Warranty claims and Extended Warranty inquiries, consistent with our Warranty Policy.

3.5 **Security Team:** security@shopsphere.com — for suspected account compromise, phishing reports, and fraud concerns, consistent with our Safe Shopping Guide.

3.6 **Privacy and Data Protection Office:** privacy@shopsphere.com — for data access, correction, deletion requests, and privacy concerns, consistent with our Privacy Policy.

3.7 **Marketplace Integrity Team:** marketplace-integrity@shopsphere.com — for reporting counterfeit goods, misleading listings, or Marketplace Seller misconduct.

3.8 **Grievance Officer:** grievance@shopsphere.com — for formal complaints unresolved through standard support channels, per our Grievance Policy.

3.9 **Legal Department:** legal@shopsphere.com — for legal notices, intellectual property complaints, and formal legal correspondence, per Section 22 of our Terms & Conditions.

## 4. Email Response Times

4.1 ShopSphere aims to meet the following response time targets for email inquiries, measured from receipt during business hours:

| Priority Level | Initial Acknowledgment | Resolution Target |
|---|---|---|
| Urgent (fraud, security, account compromise) | Within 2 hours | Within 24 hours |
| High (payment failures, delivery delays past SLA) | Within 6 hours | Within 48 hours |
| Standard (returns, refunds, general queries) | Within 24 hours | Within 3–5 business days |
| Low (feedback, general suggestions) | Within 48 hours | As applicable |

4.2 Complex cases, such as those requiring Courier Partner or Marketplace Seller investigation, may take longer than the above targets; you will be kept informed of progress at each stage.

4.3 Emails received outside business hours are logged upon receipt, and the response clock begins at the start of the next business day, except for Urgent priority issues, which are monitored continuously.

## 5. Phone Support

5.1 **Customer Support Helpline:** +91-80-4567-8900, available daily from 8:00 AM to 10:00 PM Indian Standard Time (IST).

5.2 **Priority Support Line (for Premium/Membership Users):** A dedicated priority line number is provided to eligible Users upon subscription confirmation, offering reduced wait times.

5.3 Phone support is recommended for time-sensitive issues, such as same-day delivery problems, urgent Order corrections, or when you need real-time guidance through a troubleshooting process.

5.4 Average wait times during business hours are typically under 5 minutes; wait times may increase during peak shopping periods (such as major sale events), during which additional queue capacity is deployed.

5.5 For security purposes, phone support representatives will verify your identity using your registered Account details before discussing Order or Account-specific information, consistent with Section 8.2 of our Privacy Policy regarding identity verification for data requests.

## 6. Live Chat

6.1 Live Chat is available 24/7 through the Platform (website and mobile app), accessible via the "Help" or "Chat with Us" icon.

6.2 Live Chat is initially handled by an automated assistant capable of resolving common queries such as Order tracking, Return initiation, and basic troubleshooting. You may request to be transferred to a human support agent at any time by typing "agent" or selecting the relevant option.

6.3 During business hours (8:00 AM – 10:00 PM IST), average wait time to connect with a human agent is typically under 3 minutes. Outside these hours, live chat is handled by our overnight support team with a target connection time of under 10 minutes, or you may leave a message for callback during business hours.

6.4 Live Chat transcripts are saved to your Account and can be reviewed under "My Support Tickets" for reference.

## 7. Social Media Support

7.1 ShopSphere maintains official social media presence for general inquiries and brand updates. Official channels include:

  7.1.1 Twitter/X: @ShopSphereHelp
  7.1.2 Facebook: facebook.com/ShopSphereOfficial
  7.1.3 Instagram: @ShopSphere.Official

7.2 Social media channels are monitored during business hours (9:00 AM – 9:00 PM IST) and are best suited for general questions or visibility on urgent public issues; we do not process Account-specific or payment-sensitive details over public social media posts. Direct messages may be used to route you to secure support channels.

7.3 Beware of fake or unofficial ShopSphere social media accounts, particularly those offering unrealistic discounts or requesting personal information; only trust accounts with the verified badge and handles listed above. See our Safe Shopping Guide for more on identifying impersonation attempts.

7.4 Typical response time on social media is within 4 business hours for direct messages and comments.

## 9. Escalation Matrix

9.1 If your issue is not resolved through initial contact, ShopSphere follows a tiered escalation process to ensure timely resolution:

| Level | Contact Point | When to Escalate | Target Response |
|---|---|---|---|
| Level 1 | Live Chat / General Support (support@shopsphere.com) | First point of contact for any issue | Per Section 4 response targets |
| Level 2 | Team Lead / Senior Support Agent (escalations@shopsphere.com) | Unresolved after Level 1 response time has elapsed, or unsatisfactory resolution | Within 48 hours of escalation |
| Level 3 | Grievance Officer (grievance@shopsphere.com) | Unresolved after Level 2, or for formal complaints | Per SLAs in our Grievance Policy |
| Level 4 | Nodal Officer / Senior Management (nodal-officer@shopsphere.com) | Unresolved after Grievance Officer review, or matters involving regulatory/legal significance | Within 7 business days |
| External | Consumer Disputes Redressal Commission / Regulatory Authority | If unresolved after Level 4, or at any time as per your statutory rights | Per applicable law |

9.2 You are not required to exhaust internal escalation levels before exercising your statutory rights, including approaching a consumer forum under the Consumer Protection Act, 2019; however, we encourage using our internal escalation process first, as it is often the fastest path to resolution.

9.3 Each escalation is logged with a unique ticket reference number, which you should retain and reference in all further communications regarding that issue.

9.4 For a full description of the formal complaint process, timelines, and your consumer rights, please refer to our Grievance Policy.

## 10. Order-Specific Contact Guidance

10.1 For fastest resolution, please have your Order number, registered email or phone number, and a brief description of the issue ready when contacting support through any channel.

10.2 Depending on your issue type, please refer to the following before contacting us, as many queries can be resolved via self-service:

  10.2.1 Order tracking and delivery status: "My Orders" in your Account;

  10.2.2 Return or Exchange initiation: "My Orders" > "Return" option, per our Return Policy;

  10.2.3 Refund status: "My Orders" > "Refund Status," per our Refund Policy;

  10.2.4 Warranty claims: "My Orders" > "Warranty Claim," per our Warranty Policy;

  10.2.5 Invoice download: "My Orders" > "Download Invoice."
