# ShopSphere Privacy Policy

**Effective Date: July 23, 2026**
**Last Updated: July 23, 2026**

## 1. Introduction

1.1 ShopSphere Commerce Pvt. Ltd. and its affiliates (collectively, "ShopSphere," "we," "us," or "our") are committed to protecting the privacy of individuals who visit or use www.shopsphere.com, our mobile applications, and related services (collectively, the "Platform").

1.2 This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, store, and protect your Personal Data when you access or use the Platform, and describes your rights and choices regarding your Personal Data.

1.3 This Privacy Policy should be read together with our Terms & Conditions, our Cookie Policy, and our Product Availability Policy, all of which are incorporated by reference and available on the Platform.

1.4 By accessing or using the Platform, you acknowledge that you have read and understood this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree with our practices, please do not use the Platform.

## 2. Definitions

2.1 "Personal Data" means any information relating to an identified or identifiable natural person, including but not limited to name, contact details, financial information, location data, and online identifiers.

2.2 "Sensitive Personal Data" means categories of Personal Data requiring additional protection under applicable law, including financial information (such as card or bank details), government-issued identification numbers, health data, biometric data, and passwords.

2.3 "Processing" means any operation performed on Personal Data, including collection, recording, organization, storage, adaptation, retrieval, use, disclosure, or erasure.

2.4 "Data Controller" means the entity that determines the purposes and means of Processing Personal Data. For most Processing activities described in this Policy, ShopSphere acts as the Data Controller.

2.5 "Data Processor" means an entity that processes Personal Data on behalf of a Data Controller, such as our logistics, payment, and technology service providers.

2.6 "Cookies" has the meaning given in our Cookie Policy.

2.7 "User," "you," and "your" have the same meaning as in our Terms & Conditions.

2.8 "Marketplace Seller" has the meaning given in our Terms & Conditions.

## 3. Scope and Applicability

3.1 This Privacy Policy applies to Personal Data collected through: (a) the Platform, including our website and mobile applications; (b) customer support interactions via phone, email, or chat; (c) marketing communications and surveys; and (d) offline interactions where you provide Personal Data directly to ShopSphere, such as at promotional events.

3.2 This Privacy Policy does not apply to the practices of Marketplace Sellers, third-party payment processors, logistics partners, or other Third-Party Services, which maintain their own privacy policies. We encourage you to review those policies, as further described in Section 9.

## 4. Personal Data We Collect

4.1 **Information You Provide Directly.**

  4.1.1 **Account and Registration Data:** Name, email address, phone number, date of birth (where required for age verification), password, and profile preferences.

  4.1.2 **Order and Transaction Data:** Billing and shipping addresses, Order history, Product preferences, gift messages, and communications with Marketplace Sellers or customer support.

  4.1.3 **Payment Data:** Payment card details (processed and stored by our PCI-DSS compliant payment processors, not directly by ShopSphere), UPI identifiers, billing address, and bank account details for refunds, where applicable.

  4.1.4 **User Content:** Reviews, ratings, product questions, images, and other content you submit to the Platform.

  4.1.5 **Communications:** Information you provide when contacting customer support, participating in surveys, or entering promotions, contests, or sweepstakes.

4.2 **Information Collected Automatically.**

  4.2.1 **Device and Usage Data:** IP address, device identifiers, browser type and version, operating system, referring URLs, pages viewed, time spent on pages, click-stream data, and search queries.

  4.2.2 **Location Data:** Approximate geolocation derived from your IP address, or precise geolocation if you enable location services on your device and grant permission, used to provide location-relevant Product availability, shipping estimates, and localized pricing.

  4.2.3 **Cookies and Similar Technologies:** Information collected through cookies, web beacons, pixel tags, and similar tracking technologies, as described in detail in our Cookie Policy.

4.3 **Information from Third Parties.**

  4.3.1 We may receive Personal Data about you from Marketplace Sellers (such as order-related communications), payment processors (such as payment confirmation status), logistics partners (such as delivery status), social media platforms (if you choose to log in or interact with us via social media integrations), credit bureaus or fraud-prevention services (for fraud detection and credit assessment purposes, where applicable), and publicly available sources.

4.4 **Inferred Data.** We may generate inferences about your preferences, interests, and likely Product interests based on your interactions with the Platform, for purposes of personalization and marketing as described in Section 5.

## 5. How We Use Your Personal Data

5.1 We Process your Personal Data for the following purposes, based on the legal grounds indicated:

  5.1.1 **Contract Performance:** To create and manage your Account, process and fulfill Orders, facilitate payments, arrange shipping and delivery, process returns and refunds, and provide customer support — necessary for performance of our contract with you under the Terms & Conditions.

  5.1.2 **Legitimate Interests:** To personalize your experience on the Platform, provide Product recommendations, conduct analytics to improve our Services, detect and prevent fraud and abuse, enforce our Terms & Conditions, and maintain the security and integrity of the Platform.

  5.1.3 **Consent:** To send you marketing communications, including promotional emails, SMS, and push notifications, and to use certain non-essential cookies and tracking technologies, as described in our Cookie Policy. You may withdraw consent at any time as described in Section 8.

  5.1.4 **Legal Obligation:** To comply with applicable tax, accounting, consumer protection, and other legal or regulatory requirements, including responding to lawful requests from public authorities.

5.2 Specific uses include: verifying your identity and eligibility under Section 3 of our Terms & Conditions; calculating and displaying applicable Taxes at checkout; generating tax invoices; communicating Order status and delivery updates; investigating and resolving disputes, complaints, or claims; and conducting internal research and development to improve Platform functionality.

5.3 We do not use Sensitive Personal Data, such as full payment card numbers, for marketing or profiling purposes.

## 6. Data Collection Practices and Retention

6.1 We collect Personal Data at the point you interact with the Platform, including at registration, checkout, when submitting reviews, and when contacting customer support, as well as passively through your use of the Platform as described in Section 4.2.

6.2 **Retention Periods.** We retain Personal Data for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes described in this Policy, including:

  6.2.1 Account data is retained for the duration of your Account's existence and for a reasonable period thereafter to address potential disputes or claims;

  6.2.2 Order and transaction data, including tax invoices, is retained for a minimum period as required under applicable tax and accounting laws, typically seven (7) years;

  6.2.3 Payment-related metadata is retained in accordance with applicable financial regulations and PCI-DSS requirements;

  6.2.4 Marketing preference data is retained until you withdraw consent or opt out, plus a reasonable period to give effect to your request;

  6.2.5 Customer support communications are retained for a period sufficient to resolve related issues and for quality assurance purposes, typically up to three (3) years.

6.3 Upon expiry of applicable retention periods, we will securely delete, anonymize, or aggregate your Personal Data such that it can no longer be used to identify you, unless retention is required by law.

## 7. How We Share Your Personal Data

7.1 We do not sell your Personal Data to third parties for monetary consideration. We may share your Personal Data in the following circumstances:

  7.1.1 **Marketplace Sellers:** When you purchase a Product from a Marketplace Seller, we share necessary Order and shipping information (such as your name, address, and contact details) with that Marketplace Seller to enable Order fulfillment.

  7.1.2 **Service Providers (Data Processors):** We engage third-party service providers to perform functions on our behalf, including payment processing, logistics and delivery, cloud hosting, customer support, marketing and analytics, and fraud prevention. These providers are contractually bound to Process Personal Data only as instructed by us and to implement appropriate security measures.

  7.1.3 **Payment Processors:** As described in our Terms & Conditions, payment processing is handled by licensed third-party payment gateways, which receive your payment information directly to process transactions in accordance with PCI-DSS standards.

  7.1.4 **Business Transfers:** In connection with a merger, acquisition, reorganization, sale of assets, or bankruptcy, Personal Data may be transferred as part of that transaction, subject to standard confidentiality arrangements.

  7.1.5 **Legal Compliance:** We may disclose Personal Data where required by law, regulation, legal process, or governmental request, or where we believe disclosure is necessary to protect the rights, property, or safety of ShopSphere, our Users, or the public.

  7.1.6 **With Your Consent:** We may share Personal Data with other third parties where you have provided explicit consent to do so.

  7.1.7 **Affiliates:** We may share Personal Data among ShopSphere's corporate affiliates and subsidiaries for the purposes described in this Policy.

7.2 We require all third parties with whom we share Personal Data to maintain the confidentiality and security of that data and to use it only for the purposes for which it was disclosed, consistent with this Policy and applicable law.

## 8. Your Rights and Choices

8.1 Subject to applicable law, you have the following rights regarding your Personal Data:

  8.1.1 **Right to Access:** You may request confirmation of whether we Process your Personal Data and request a copy of that data.

  8.1.2 **Right to Rectification:** You may request correction of inaccurate or incomplete Personal Data, which you can generally do directly through your Account Settings.

  8.1.3 **Right to Erasure:** You may request deletion of your Personal Data, subject to our legitimate need to retain certain data for legal, tax, fraud-prevention, or dispute-resolution purposes as described in Section 6.2.

  8.1.4 **Right to Restrict or Object to Processing:** You may object to Processing based on legitimate interests, including for direct marketing purposes, and may request restriction of Processing in certain circumstances.

  8.1.5 **Right to Data Portability:** Where technically feasible, you may request a copy of your Personal Data in a structured, commonly used, machine-readable format.

  8.1.6 **Right to Withdraw Consent:** Where Processing is based on your consent, you may withdraw that consent at any time, without affecting the lawfulness of Processing carried out prior to withdrawal.

  8.1.7 **Right to Opt Out of Marketing:** You may opt out of marketing communications at any time by using the "unsubscribe" link in emails, adjusting notification preferences in your Account Settings, or contacting us at privacy@shopsphere.com. Note that you will continue to receive transactional and Order-related communications necessary for Service delivery, even after opting out of marketing.

  8.1.8 **Right to Lodge a Complaint:** You have the right to lodge a complaint with a competent data protection authority in your jurisdiction if you believe our Processing of your Personal Data violates applicable law.

8.2 To exercise any of these rights, please submit a request to privacy@shopsphere.com or through the "Privacy Requests" section of your Account Settings. We will verify your identity before processing your request and will respond within the timeframe required by applicable law, generally within thirty (30) days.

8.3 We may decline or limit certain requests where permitted by law, such as where fulfilling the request would infringe upon the rights of another individual, conflict with legal retention obligations, or where the request is manifestly unfounded or excessive.

## 10. Data Security

10.1 ShopSphere implements reasonable and appropriate technical and organizational security measures designed to protect your Personal Data against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction, including:

  10.1.1 Encryption of data in transit using Transport Layer Security (TLS) protocols;

  10.1.2 Encryption of sensitive data at rest;

  10.1.3 Access controls limiting Personal Data access to authorized personnel on a need-to-know basis;

  10.1.4 Regular security assessments, vulnerability scanning, and penetration testing;

  10.1.5 Multi-factor authentication options for Account access;

  10.1.6 PCI-DSS compliant handling of payment card data through certified third-party processors;

  10.1.7 Employee training on data protection and confidentiality obligations.

10.2 Despite these measures, no method of electronic transmission or storage is completely secure, and ShopSphere cannot guarantee absolute security of your Personal Data. You are responsible for maintaining the confidentiality of your Account credentials, as described in Section 4.2 of our Terms & Conditions.

10.3 **Data Breach Notification.** In the event of a Personal Data breach that poses a risk to your rights and freedoms, ShopSphere will notify affected Users and applicable regulatory authorities without undue delay, in accordance with applicable law.
