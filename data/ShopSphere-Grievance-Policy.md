# ShopSphere Grievance Policy

**Effective Date: July 23, 2026**
**Last Updated: July 23, 2026**

## 1. Introduction

1.1 This Grievance Policy sets out the process by which ShopSphere Commerce Pvt. Ltd. ("ShopSphere," "we," "us," or "our") receives, addresses, and resolves complaints and grievances raised by Users of www.shopsphere.com and our mobile applications (collectively, the "Platform"), in accordance with applicable consumer protection and information technology laws, including the Consumer Protection (E-Commerce) Rules, 2020 and the Information Technology Act, 2000, as amended.

1.2 This Policy should be read together with our Terms & Conditions (particularly Section 18, Dispute Resolution), Privacy Policy, Contact Us page, and FAQ, all of which are available on the Platform.

1.3 This Policy applies to grievances relating to Orders, Products, Marketplace Sellers, Platform functionality, data privacy, and any other matter arising from your use of the Platform.

## 2. Definitions

2.1 "Complaint" or "Grievance" means any expression of dissatisfaction made by a User to ShopSphere related to its Products, Services, or the complaint-handling process itself, where a response or resolution is explicitly or implicitly expected.

2.2 "Grievance Officer" means the individual designated by ShopSphere to receive, process, and resolve Grievances in accordance with applicable law and this Policy.

2.3 "Nodal Officer" means a senior officer designated to handle escalated Grievances and coordinate with law enforcement or regulatory authorities as required.

2.4 "Service Level Agreement" (SLA) means the target timelines described in this Policy for acknowledging and resolving Grievances.

2.5 Capitalized terms not otherwise defined in this Policy have the meanings given to them in our Terms & Conditions.

## 3. Scope of Grievances Covered

3.1 This Policy covers Grievances relating to, including but not limited to:

  3.1.1 Order fulfillment issues, including delayed, lost, or damaged Shipments;

  3.1.2 Return, Refund, Exchange, or Cancellation disputes;

  3.1.3 Warranty claim disputes;

  3.1.4 Marketplace Seller conduct, including counterfeit goods, misleading listings, or non-fulfillment;

  3.1.5 Billing, payment, or pricing discrepancies;

  3.1.6 Privacy and data protection concerns, including requests that remain unresolved through the standard privacy request process described in Section 8 of our Privacy Policy;

  3.1.7 Content-related grievances, including objectionable User Content or intermediary liability matters under applicable law;

  3.1.8 Suspected fraud, unauthorized transactions, or account security incidents;

  3.1.9 General dissatisfaction with Platform functionality, customer service, or policy application.

## 4. Complaint Process

4.1 **Step 1 — Standard Support Channels.** Most issues should first be raised through our standard support channels described in our Contact Us page, including Live Chat, email (support@shopsphere.com), or phone support. Many Grievances can be resolved at this stage without formal escalation.

4.2 **Step 2 — Formal Grievance Submission.** If your issue remains unresolved after Step 1, or if you wish to raise a formal Grievance directly, you may submit it through any of the following methods:

  4.2.1 Email to grievance@shopsphere.com, including your Order number (if applicable), a description of the issue, any supporting documentation (such as photographs or screenshots), and your desired resolution;

  4.2.2 The "Raise a Grievance" form available in the Help Center section of the Platform;

  4.2.3 Written correspondence to our registered office address, addressed to the Grievance Officer.

4.3 **Step 3 — Acknowledgment.** Upon receipt, your Grievance will be logged and assigned a unique reference number, and you will receive an acknowledgment in accordance with the SLA described in Section 5.

4.4 **Step 4 — Investigation.** The Grievance Officer or their designated team will investigate the matter, which may involve reviewing Order records, communicating with the relevant Marketplace Seller or Courier Partner, and requesting additional information from you where necessary.

4.5 **Step 5 — Resolution.** You will be notified of the outcome of the investigation and any remedy offered, which may include a refund, replacement, Account correction, policy clarification, or other appropriate action consistent with our Terms & Conditions and related policies.

4.6 **Step 6 — Escalation (if needed).** If you are not satisfied with the resolution provided, you may escalate the matter as described in Section 7 (Escalation) of this Policy.

## 5. Service Level Agreements (SLAs)

5.1 ShopSphere aims to meet the following SLA targets for Grievance handling, measured in business days from receipt:

| Grievance Type | Acknowledgment | Resolution Target |
|---|---|---|
| Order/delivery issues (delay, damage, loss) | Within 24 hours | Within 5 business days |
| Return/Refund/Cancellation disputes | Within 24 hours | Within 5–7 business days |
| Warranty claim disputes | Within 48 hours | Within 10 business days (subject to Authorized Service Center timelines) |
| Marketplace Seller conduct complaints | Within 48 hours | Within 7 business days |
| Payment/billing discrepancies | Within 24 hours | Within 5 business days |
| Privacy/data protection requests | Within 48 hours | Within 30 days (per applicable data protection law) |
| Fraud/security incidents | Within 2 hours (Urgent) | Within 3–5 business days |
| Content/intermediary liability grievances | Within 24 hours | Within 15 business days (per applicable IT law) |
| General Platform/service dissatisfaction | Within 48 hours | Within 10 business days |

5.2 Where a Grievance involves third parties, such as Courier Partners or Marketplace Sellers, resolution may take longer than the stated targets if additional investigation time is genuinely required; in such cases, we will provide interim status updates at least every 5 business days until resolution.

5.3 In accordance with applicable Indian e-commerce regulations, ShopSphere's Grievance Officer will acknowledge receipt of any Grievance within forty-eight (48) hours and endeavor to redress it within one (1) month from the date of receipt, except where a shorter timeline is specified above for a particular Grievance type.

## 8. Dispute Handling and Consumer Rights

8.1 **Internal Resolution First.** ShopSphere encourages Users to attempt resolution through the internal Grievance process described in this Policy before pursuing external remedies, as this is often the fastest path to a satisfactory outcome. This is consistent with the informal resolution requirement described in Section 18.1 of our Terms & Conditions.

8.2 **Arbitration.** Where a Grievance cannot be resolved internally and constitutes a formal legal dispute, either party may refer the matter to binding arbitration in accordance with Section 18.2 of our Terms & Conditions, seated in Bengaluru, India, under the Arbitration and Conciliation Act, 1996.

8.3 **Consumer Forum Rights.** Regardless of the outcome of ShopSphere's internal Grievance process, you retain the right, at any time, to file a complaint before the appropriate District, State, or National Consumer Disputes Redressal Commission under the Consumer Protection Act, 2019, or to approach any other competent regulatory or judicial authority, as described in Section 18.5 of our Terms & Conditions. Nothing in this Policy limits or delays your access to such statutory remedies.

8.4 **National Consumer Helpline.** You may also register complaints through India's National Consumer Helpline (toll-free: 1915, or via the UMANG app / National Consumer Helpline website), which serves as an alternate dispute resolution mechanism recognized under Indian consumer protection law.

8.5 **Rights Under the Consumer Protection Act, 2019.** As a consumer, you have rights including the right to be protected against unsafe or hazardous goods, the right to be informed of the quality, quantity, and price of goods, the right to be heard and assured that your interests will receive due consideration, and the right to seek redressal against unfair or restrictive trade practices. ShopSphere is committed to upholding these rights in its dealings with Users.

8.6 **E-Commerce Specific Obligations.** Consistent with the Consumer Protection (E-Commerce) Rules, 2020, ShopSphere ensures clear display of seller details, grievance mechanisms, and return/refund/exchange terms on Product listing pages, and does not engage in practices such as manipulation of price to gain unreasonable profit through unjustified price rise or falsely representing goods as being of a particular standard.

## 9. Grievances Relating to Marketplace Sellers

9.1 Where a Grievance concerns a Product sold by a Marketplace Seller, ShopSphere, in its role as an intermediary Platform as described in Section 10.3 of our Terms & Conditions, will investigate the matter, seek necessary information from the Marketplace Seller, and facilitate an appropriate remedy consistent with our Return, Refund, and Warranty Policies.

9.2 Where a Grievance concerns suspected counterfeit goods, misleading listings, or repeated Marketplace Seller misconduct, it will additionally be referred to our Marketplace Integrity team (marketplace-integrity@shopsphere.com) for investigation and potential action against the Marketplace Seller's Platform access, independent of the individual Grievance resolution provided to you.

## 10. Confidentiality

10.1 Information shared as part of a Grievance will be handled in accordance with our Privacy Policy and used solely for the purposes of investigating and resolving the Grievance, coordinating with relevant internal teams, Marketplace Sellers, or Courier Partners as necessary, and improving our Services.

## 11. Record-Keeping

11.1 ShopSphere maintains records of Grievances received, including acknowledgment dates, investigation notes, and resolution outcomes, for a period consistent with our data retention practices described in Section 6.2 of our Privacy Policy, and as may be required for regulatory compliance and reporting purposes.
